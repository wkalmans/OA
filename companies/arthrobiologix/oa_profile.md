# ArthroBiologix Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Hyaluronic acid + corticosteroid injection combination product
**Mechanism:** Combined intra-articular injection pairing hyaluronic acid (joint lubrication/viscosupplementation) with a corticosteroid (short-term anti-inflammatory) for joint pain.

## Current Status
ArthroBiologix is a small orthobiologics-focused research outfit; no dedicated company website or press coverage was found. The company's trials are procedural/device-style studies rather than novel-drug development: it also runs a separate platelet-rich-plasma (PRP) knee OA study (NCT04111718, since 2019) and a trial (NON-OP HIP) testing whether combining a corticosteroid + hyaluronic acid injection with physical therapy improves outcomes versus injection alone in hip OA/impingement patients. No results, funding news, or partnerships were found beyond the ClinicalTrials.gov listings themselves.
