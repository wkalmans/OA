# Pacira BioSciences — OA Asset Profile: ZILRETTA & PCRX-201

*Compiled 2026-08-21. Covers SEC filings through the Q2 2026 10-Q (filed 2026-08-04), PubMed literature, and public news through August 2026.*

---

## Asset Overview

### ZILRETTA (triamcinolone acetonide extended-release injectable suspension)
- FDA-approved **October 6, 2017** — first and only extended-release intra-articular corticosteroid for osteoarthritis (OA) knee pain. Acquired via Pacira's 2021 acquisition of Flexion Therapeutics.
- Mechanism: proprietary PLGA (poly lactic-co-glycolic acid) microsphere technology combined with triamcinolone acetonide, designed to provide pain relief for ~12 weeks (some patients through week 16) per the pivotal Phase 3 trial.
- Commercial status: marketed, U.S.-only. FY2025 net product sales **$116.6 million**, roughly 16% of Pacira's total company revenue ($726.4M). It is Pacira's second-largest product behind EXPAREL (~79% of revenue).
- U.S. composition-of-matter and method patents run to **2031**; no FDA-approved generic as of this writing. (Note: Pacira's active ANDA/Paragraph IV patent litigation with WhiteOak Group and Qilu Pharmaceutical, disclosed October–November 2025, concerns **EXPAREL**, not ZILRETTA — the two are sometimes conflated in secondary sources.)
  [10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1396814/000162828026012297/pcrx-20251231.htm)
- Label-expansion effort: a Phase 3 registrational trial of ZILRETTA for **OA pain of the shoulder** (launched 2024) — would be the first on-label shoulder-OA treatment if successful.

### PCRX-201 (enekinragene inzadenovec)
- Investigational, locally-administered **gene therapy** for knee OA — Pacira's lead pipeline asset and lead program from its proprietary high-capacity adenovirus (HCAd) vector platform.
- Mechanism: a non-integrating, non-replicating adenovirus serotype 5 vector, delivered via a single intra-articular injection, that turns joint cells into local factories producing IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra) under an inflammation-responsive promoter — intended to suppress chronic IL-1-driven joint inflammation without systemic exposure.
- Origin: licensed from GQ Bio Therapeutics (2017, via Flexion); Pacira acquired the remaining 81% of GQ Bio it didn't already own in **February 2025** for $30.6 million net, bringing the HCAd platform fully in-house.
- Regulatory designations: FDA **RMAT** (Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy, granted February 2024) and EMA **ATMP** (Advanced Therapy Medicinal Product) — the first gene therapy in OA to receive both.
- Development stage: **Phase 2** ("ASCEND" study, NCT06884865) — a two-part, randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial targeting ~135 patients total (Part A ~45 planned / 49 actually randomized; Part B ~90 planned). Per ClinicalTrials.gov, status is listed **Recruiting** as of the August 7, 2026 registry update, with an estimated primary completion date of **November 2026**.
  [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06884865](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06884865)
- Phase 1 (NCT04119687, 72 patients) generated 2-year (reported November 2024) and 3-year (reported June and October 2025) durability data showing sustained pain/stiffness/function improvement at all three doses tested.

## Timeline of Public Statements ("Said")

- **2024 (year, exact date not specified in filings):** Pacira states it "launched a Phase 3 trial investigating ZILRETTA as a treatment for OA pain of the shoulder," comparing ZILRETTA to placebo. [10-K FY2025, p.13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1396814/000162828026012297/pcrx-20251231.htm)
- **February 2024:** FDA grants PCRX-201 RMAT designation for knee OA — the first gene therapy to receive RMAT in this indication. [10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1396814/000162828026012297/pcrx-20251231.htm)

## Discrepancies / Watch Items

1. **5x30 "double-digit CAGR" target vs. ZILRETTA's actual FY2025 revenue decline.** The 5x30 plan (announced January 2025) commits to a double-digit product-revenue CAGR by 2030; in the plan's first reported full year, ZILRETTA revenue fell 1% and total company revenue grew only 4%. 2026 quarters show ZILRETTA growth reaccelerating (+15% Q1, +4% Q2), but it's too early to call the multi-year trend re-established.
2. **ZILRETTA shoulder-OA trial: enrollment pause → $25.9M impairment → still no topline data.** The trial launched in 2024 with a straightforward "interim analysis will inform next steps" framing; by Q3 2025 it had an undisclosed-duration enrollment pause tied to an IRB protocol-amendment issue, coincident with a $25.9M non-cash impairment explicitly citing "revised completion timelines." Enrollment didn't conclude until April 2026, and topline data remained outstanding as of the August 2026 quarterly filing. This is the single largest financial marker in the filings of a said/did gap on an OA-specific program.
3. **PCRX-201 ASCEND Part A "completion" was announced twice, ~7 months apart.** Pacira told investors in November 2025 (via dedicated press release and the Q3 2025 10-Q) that Part A enrollment had concluded. Filings from July–August 2026 instead state enrollment "concluded in June 2026" with a higher final patient count (49 vs. the original ~45 target). The filings do not explicitly reconcile these two "completion" announcements — it's unclear from the public record whether this reflects supplemental enrollment tied to the switch to the new commercial-scale manufacturing process, a data-quality/protocol issue, or an administrative restatement. Worth flagging for the next quarterly filing to see if management addresses it directly (e.g., on an earnings call).
4. **No standalone forward-looking $ guidance for ZILRETTA.** Pacira gives explicit annual guidance for EXPAREL net sales and total company revenue, but not for ZILRETTA specifically — makes it harder to hold management to a specific number for this asset; investors must back into an implied range.
5. **ZILRETTA patent cliff is 2031, not imminent** — no ANDA/generic filing against ZILRETTA identified in filings or news as of August 2026 (the active WhiteOak/Qilu Paragraph IV litigation concerns EXPAREL only). Flagging as a "watch" item for the next data pull, not a current discrepancy.
6. **PCRX-1002/PCRX-1003/PCRX-2002 are not OA assets** — worth noting to avoid future confusion: PCRX-2002 (in-licensed from AmacaThera, Q4 2025, $5.0M upfront) is a postsurgical-pain asset (EXPAREL-adjacent), and PCRX-1002/1003 (dry eye, degenerative disc disease) are other HCAd-platform preclinical programs — none of these are knee/shoulder OA programs, distinct from PCRX-201.

## Key Scientific Literature

- **PCRX-201 Phase 1, 2-year results.** Cohen S, Hochberg MC, Kivitz A, et al. "Safety, biodistribution, and exploratory clinical outcomes of a novel intra-articular IL-1Ra gene therapy (PCRX-201) in moderate-to-severe knee osteoarthritis: 2-year results of a phase 1 study." *Ann Rheum Dis.* 2026 Aug 4 (online ahead of print). Open-label Phase 1 (NCT04119687), 72 patients, 3 dose levels ± glucocorticoid pretreatment. Transient knee effusion was the most common AE (36% with steroid pretreatment vs. 61% without); very limited biodistribution outside the knee; by week 104, 33/72 (45.8%) had discontinued the study, but those remaining showed sustained pain/stiffness/function improvement across doses and cohorts regardless of pre-existing neutralizing antibodies. PMID: [42552219](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42552219/). DOI: 10.1016/j.ard.2026.06.034. Four Pacira employees are co-authors.
- **ZILRETTA (TA-ER) in knee OA patients with type 2 diabetes.** Spitzer AI, Rodbard H. "Plain Language Summary of a Phase 2 Study of Triamcinolone Acetonide Extended-Release for Pain Relief in People With Knee Osteoarthritis and Type 2 Diabetes." *Ther Adv Musculoskelet Dis.* 2026 Apr 9. Plain-language summary of a Pacira-sponsored study comparing TA-ER (ZILRETTA) to standard crystalline triamcinolone acetonide; TA-ER produced smaller and fewer blood-glucose spikes, no serious AEs in either arm. PMID: [41978715](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41978715/). PMCID: PMC13070166.
- **Triamcinolone acetonide cartilage-safety debate (general TA, not ZILRETTA-branded, but directly relevant to the corticosteroid-in-OA safety question ZILRETTA competes on).** Porter A, Nguyen J, Peng Y, et al. "Triamcinolone Acetonide Is Not Harmful to Healthy and Osteoarthritic Human Knee Cartilage." *Am J Sports Med.* 2026 Jul;54(8):1944-1953. Ex vivo human cartilage study; TA did not reduce chondrocyte viability at clinically relevant concentrations; some reversible suppression of matrix synthesis only at supra-clinical, continuous 14-day exposure. PMID: [42011976](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42011976/).
- **Response/rebuttal letter.** Liu Q, Du H. "Revisiting the Chondrotoxicity of Intra-Articular Triamcinolone Acetonide in Osteoarthritis: Dose Dependence and Experimental Model Heterogeneity" [Letter]. *J Pain Res.* 2026 Apr 17. PMID: [42022496](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42022496/). PMCID: PMC13098681.
- **PCRX-201 3-year Phase 1 durability data** (not yet in a peer-reviewed journal as of this pull; presented as a conference poster). ACR Convergence 2025, Chicago, October 28, 2025 — sustained pain/stiffness/function improvement through 156 weeks across all three doses; consistent efficacy/safety across structural-severity subgroups; pre-existing neutralizing antibodies did not impact outcomes. [Investor press release](https://investor.pacira.com/news-releases/news-release-details/pacira-biosciences-presents-updated-three-year-data)
