# Scarcell Therapeutics S.A.S. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** aeGF (allogeneic engineered gingival fibroblasts)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic gingival fibroblast cell therapy, single intra-articular injection, intended to reduce pain/inflammation and support cartilage repair via immune-tolerant donor cells (no immunosuppression required).

## Current Status
Scarcell (France) is running a first-in-human Phase 1 safety/feasibility study of aeGF in knee OA (NCT06690710, recruiting); the company's own site describes this as entering Phase Ib in Europe as of mid-2025. The company cites substantial preclinical support: safety and durability data in over 800 companion animals (dogs and racehorses with naturally occurring OA), with follow-up out to two years, showing pain relief and cartilage regeneration without immunosuppressive drugs. Both the cell product and its manufacturing process reportedly hold US patent protection. No human efficacy data yet — this is an early-stage program with a stronger-than-typical preclinical/veterinary data package.
