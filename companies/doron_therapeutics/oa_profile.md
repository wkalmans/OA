# Doron Therapeutics Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** PTP-01 (PTP-001), branded MOTYS
**Mechanism:** Terminally sterilized, off-the-shelf regenerative biologic derived from donated placental tissue (secretome, growth factors, extracellular matrix), injected intra-articularly for symptomatic knee OA.

## Current Status
Doron Therapeutics reported a positive End-of-Phase 2 / initial RMAT meeting with the FDA for MOTYS, which holds both Fast Track and RMAT designations. As of October 2025 the company dosed first patients in a randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial (~300 patients, multi-site/multi-country) measuring pain and function improvement for up to 12 months after a single injection; a related report notes the company raised $11M to fund the Phase 3 advance. This is a genuinely active, well-funded program for a company of its size.
