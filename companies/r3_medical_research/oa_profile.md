# R3 Medical Research — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Umbilical Cord Wharton's Jelly Allograft (intra-articular knee injection)
**Mechanism:** Allograft derived from umbilical cord Wharton's jelly, rich in growth factors, cytokines, hyaluronic acid, and extracellular vesicles, injected into the knee joint for regenerative/anti-inflammatory effect.

## Current Status
R3 Medical Research is a small U.S. clinical/regenerative-medicine research group. Its trial (NCT06608134) is an Early Phase 1, dose-escalation pilot study (120 patients across low/medium/high dose arms of 2–4cc) assessing safety, feasibility, pain relief, and functional improvement over one year. This is an early feasibility study, not a pivotal trial, and no results, funding, or partnership news beyond the registration were found.
