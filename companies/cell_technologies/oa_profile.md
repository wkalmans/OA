# CELL Technologies Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Autologous Bone Marrow Aspirate Concentrate (BMAC)
**Mechanism:** Point-of-care cell therapy — bone marrow drawn from the patient's pelvis, concentrated at bedside, and injected intra-articularly into the knee to deliver progenitor cells.

## Current Status
CELL Technologies Inc. is running ACCELERATE3, a Pan-Canadian, Phase 3, open-label, randomized trial (374 planned patients) comparing a single intra-articular BMAC injection against standard of care in mild-to-severe knee osteoarthritis, with 48-week follow-up. Riam Shamma, MD is listed as Principal Investigator. This is a procedure/device-style autologous therapy rather than a manufactured biologic, so there is no separate drug-approval pathway news to report; no additional public disclosure (funding, results, partnerships) was found beyond the trial listing itself.
