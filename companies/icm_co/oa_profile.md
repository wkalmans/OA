# ICM Co. Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** ICM-203
**Mechanism:** Intra-articular AAV gene therapy delivering a truncated form of human Nkx3.2, a transcription factor supporting chondrocyte and synoviocyte activity, to protect/restore cartilage and synovium.

## Current Status
ICM-203 is in a Phase 1/2a, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-escalation study in mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis (NCT05454566), with a companion long-term follow-up study (NCT05752032) tracking safety/efficacy for up to 5 years post-dose. Interim data presented at OARSI (published via Osteoarthritis and Cartilage/ScienceDirect) reported knee pain reduction in 6 of 8 low-dose-cohort subjects and function improvement in 4 of 8 through Week 52, with cartilage thickness preserved or improved in 5 of 8 and bone marrow lesions improved in 3 of 8 — an early but encouraging signal from a small first-in-human cohort.
