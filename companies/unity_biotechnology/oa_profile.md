# Unity Biotechnology, Inc. — OA Pipeline Profile

*Lighter-touch pull. Last researched 2026-08-21.*

## Asset Overview

- **UBX0101** — a small-molecule senolytic (p53/MDM2 interaction inhibitor) designed to selectively clear senescent ("zombie") cells that accumulate in osteoarthritic joints. Delivered as an intra-articular injection for painful knee OA.
- Senolysis was, at the time, considered a genuinely novel disease-modifying mechanism for OA — clearing senescent cells rather than just treating symptoms — and UBX0101 was the most clinically advanced senolytic in the OA space.
- **Phase 1** (open-label, small n) suggested a dose-dependent reduction in knee pain in older OA patients.
- **Phase 2** (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled) reported topline 12-week data in **August 2020**: no statistically significant difference between any UBX0101 dose arm and placebo on WOMAC-A pain change from baseline — the trial **missed its primary endpoint**. Unity attributed part of the miss to an unusually large placebo response. [GlobeNewswire, 2020-08-17](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/08/17/2079116/0/en/UNITY-Biotechnology-Announces-12-week-data-from-UBX0101-Phase-2-Clinical-Study-in-Patients-with-Painful-Osteoarthritis-of-the-Knee.html)
- Following the miss, Unity said it did not plan to advance UBX0101 into pivotal studies and would narrow focus to its ophthalmology and neurology senescence programs instead. [Longevity.Technology](https://longevity.technology/news/unity-cuts-lead-program-after-clinical-trial-fail/)

## Current Status

**Unity Biotechnology no longer exists as an operating or SEC-reporting company. The OA program (UBX0101) was abandoned in 2020 and never revived.**

- The company pivoted its remaining resources into **ophthalmology** (UBX1325, a senolytic for diabetic macular edema/AMD) and early **neurology** senescence assets after the UBX0101 failure. UBX1325 actually reached Phase 2b (ASPIRE study) and reported reasonably competitive 36-week efficacy data vs. aflibercept in DME. [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/ubx1325-matches-aflibercept-in-vision-gains-for-dme-at-36-weeks); [NEJM Evidence](https://evidence.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/EVIDoa2400009)
- Despite that, on **2025-05-02** Unity's board approved a workforce reduction affecting essentially the entire company and announced it was exploring "strategic alternatives" — including sale, licensing, merger, or wind-down/dissolution — citing the need to preserve capital. The CEO, CFO, and Chief Legal Officer moved into consulting roles. [SEC 8-K, 2025-05-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000095017025063167/ubx-20250502.htm); [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/unity-biotechnology-explores-strategic-alternatives-post-aspire-study)
- Stock was subsequently **delisted** (8-Ks with Item 3.01 "Notice of Delisting," 2025-06-18 and 2025-06-27) and moved to Pink Sheets under ticker UNBX. [SEC 8-K, 2025-06-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000095017025091210/ubx-20250627.htm)
- No buyer or merger partner materialized. Stockholders voted **450M–150M in favor** of a **Plan of Complete Liquidation and Dissolution** at a special meeting on **2025-09-18**. [SEC 8-K, 2025-09-19 (Item 5.07)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000119312525209210/d804178d8k.htm)
- Unity filed a **Certificate of Dissolution** with the Delaware Secretary of State, effective **2025-09-26 at 5:00pm ET**, closed its stock transfer books, and terminated its office lease. [SEC 8-K, 2025-09-26 (Items 1.02, 3.03)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000119312525220778/d190494d8k.htm); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/sec-filings/unity-biotechnology-files-for-dissolution-and-ends-lease-agreements-93CH-4258557)
- Unity then formally **deregistered its securities with the SEC**, filing a **Form 15-12G on 2026-01-02**, terminating its Exchange Act reporting obligations. Confirmed via the SEC EDGAR company-filings feed for CIK 1463361: current record shows no active ticker and no exchange listing. [Form 15-12G, filed 2026-01-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000119312526000373/d80085d1512g.htm)
- The company's website (unitybiotechnology.com) now states plainly: **"The Company is no longer operating,"** with only a mailing address and pointer to historical SEC filings — no live pipeline or program information. [unitybiotechnology.com](https://unitybiotechnology.com)

**Bottom line for the "said vs. did" case study:** Unity never abandoned senolytics as a mechanism after the OA failure — it kept the platform alive for six more years in eye disease, where a Phase 2b readout was arguably a qualified success (non-inferior to standard of care). But the company could not translate that into a partnership, financing, or approval path, and instead of a pivot narrative ending in a new drug, it ended in full corporate dissolution and SEC deregistration in 2025–2026. The OA asset itself (UBX0101) was never revived, sold, or out-licensed as far as public records show.

## Key Scientific Literature

- **Chin AF, Han J, Clement CC, et al.** "Senolytic treatment reduces oxidative protein stress in an aging male murine model of post-traumatic osteoarthritis." *Aging Cell*, 2023 Nov;22(11):e13979. PMID: [37749958](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37749958/). Uses UBX0101 in a preclinical (mouse) model to study oxidative stress in aged joints — post-hoc academic work continuing after the clinical failure; one author discloses Unity Biotechnology equity.
- **Brandt MD, Malone JB, Kean TJ.** "Advances and Challenges in the Pursuit of Disease-Modifying Osteoarthritis Drugs: A Review of 2010-2024 Clinical Trials." *Biomedicines*, 2025 Feb 4;13(2):355. PMID: [40002768](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40002768/). Reviews 11 DMOAD candidates including UBX0101 and TissueGene-C (see Kolon TissueGene profile) as of mid-2024 — useful cross-company context; notes UBX0101 among candidates that did **not** show significant benefit.
