# VivaTech International, Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** StroMed + PRP combination
**Mechanism:** Proprietary ultrasound-cavitation process extracts stromal vascular fraction ("StroMed") cells with intact extracellular matrix from a patient's own adipose tissue (no enzymes/chemicals used), combined with autologous platelet-rich plasma and injected into the joint to reduce inflammation and support tissue repair.

## Current Status
VivaTech International (VivaTech USA) is a small regenerative-medicine company running an open-label Phase 2 trial combining StroMed and PRP for hip (and more broadly hip/shoulder/knee) osteoarthritis, initially enrolling ~50 patients for safety/efficacy before a planned expansion to up to 4,000 patients. A related published case series on combined stromal vascular fraction + PRP + exercise therapy for knee OA reported meaningful pain/function improvement, though that study is not confirmed to be the same proprietary StroMed process. No results specific to the VivaTech-sponsored trial itself were found, and no partnership/funding news beyond the company's own patient/doctor information pages.
