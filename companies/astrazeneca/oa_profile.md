# AstraZeneca — Osteoarthritis Pipeline Profile

*Light-touch pull, compiled 2026-08-21. Sources: company website/pipeline page, press coverage of pipeline updates, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed. Not a deep-dive SEC review.*

## Bottom line

AstraZeneca has **no active osteoarthritis drug pipeline today**. Its most recent and most advanced OA candidate, **MEDI7352** (an NGF/TNF-alpha bispecific fusion protein), was discontinued in Q1 2025 as part of a broader shutdown of AstraZeneca's neuroscience/pain research group. The two older candidates in our data, **MEDI-578** (an anti-NGF antibody) and **AZD1386** (a TRPV1 antagonist), were both discontinued long ago, in the early-to-mid 2010s. AstraZeneca's current public-facing pipeline page organizes the company around oncology, cardiovascular/renal/metabolic, respiratory/immunology, and rare disease — osteoarthritis and musculoskeletal disease are not listed. [AstraZeneca pipeline page](https://www.astrazeneca.com/our-therapy-areas/pipeline.html)

## Asset Overview

### MEDI7352 (NGF/TNF-alpha bispecific fusion protein)
- Confirmed mechanism: a bispecific fusion protein combining a single-chain antibody fragment (scFv) against NGF, linked via IgG1 CH2/CH3 domains to TNF receptor 2 (TNFR2), designed to neutralize both NGF and TNF-alpha signaling simultaneously — two separate pain/inflammation pathways in one molecule. [HRA study summary](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/az-d5680c00001-ph1-medi7352-in-painful-osteoarthritis-of-the-knee/)
- Progressed furthest of AstraZeneca's OA assets: Phase 1 in knee OA (NCT02508155), then a larger Phase 2b dose-response study in painful knee OA, "BESPOKE" (NCT04675034, ~345 participants), plus a parallel Phase 2 program in painful diabetic neuropathy (NCT03755934).

### MEDI-578 (anti-NGF monoclonal antibody)
- Confirmed mechanism: a monoclonal antibody that binds and neutralizes nerve growth factor (NGF), a mediator of chronic pain in arthritis. Same drug class as tanezumab/fasinumab. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01072591](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01072591)
- Reached only Phase 1 in knee OA (NCT01072591), run with MedImmune LLC (AstraZeneca's biologics unit) as collaborator, first posted Feb 2010.

### AZD1386 (TRPV1 antagonist)
- Confirmed mechanism: an orally available small-molecule antagonist of the TRPV1 (capsaicin/heat) receptor channel, explored for OA and other pain indications. [MedKoo compound page](https://www.medkoo.com/products/12352)
- Reached Phase 2 dose-finding in OA (per PubMed literature below), but development was terminated after AZD1386 caused elevated body temperature and loss of heat-pain perception — an on-target TRPV1 side effect — which limited dosing to a sub-efficacious range.

## Current Status (as of most recent data found)

**Company-wide:** AstraZeneca's current pipeline page (checked live) lists no osteoarthritis or musculoskeletal programs; therapy areas emphasized are oncology, cardiovascular/renal/metabolic, respiratory/immunology, and rare disease. This is consistent with the company's 2025 strategic exit from neuroscience/pain R&D, of which OA had been a minor part. ## Recent News

- **Apr 29, 2025** — AstraZeneca's Q1 2025 results/pipeline update confirms MEDI7352 dropped from the pipeline, alongside two other neuroscience-group assets, as the company closes that research unit to focus resources on weight management, dyslipidemia, respiratory disease, and immunology. [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/astrazeneca-exits-neuroscience-brain-drug-research/746869/); [FierceBiotech coverage](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/astrazeneca-culls-lilly-partnered-alzheimers-drug-alongside-2-other-neuroscience-meds-q1)
- **Feb 10, 2025** — Results for the MEDI7352 Phase 2b knee-OA "BESPOKE" trial (NCT04675034) posted to ClinicalTrials.gov (detailed efficacy/safety outcomes not independently verified in this pull — recommend a follow-up read of the posted results table for the actual pain-endpoint data). [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04675034](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04675034)
- **Nov 27, 2025** — A related MEDI7352 Phase 2a study in painful diabetic neuropathy (not OA) was posted as a preprint on medRxiv, discussing NGF-suppression modeling that also references the osteoarthritis program — useful background on the mechanism even though the trial itself was not in OA patients. [medRxiv preprint](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.27.25341159v1.full)

*No AstraZeneca press release specifically headlining the MEDI7352 discontinuation was found on the company's own newsroom; the news came out via routine quarterly-results pipeline disclosures and was reported by trade press (BioPharma Dive, FierceBiotech), not a standalone AZ announcement.*
