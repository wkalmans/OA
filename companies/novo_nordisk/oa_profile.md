**Last updated:** 2026-08-21
**Prepared as part of:** OA drug-development "said vs. did" tracker

---

## Asset Overview

**NNC0487-0111** is the drug-substance code for **zenagamtide** (INN),
Novo Nordisk's internal development code **NN9487**, formerly known
throughout most of its public life as **amycretin**. Novo Nordisk renamed
the molecule from "amycretin" to "zenagamtide" between its FY2025 (Q4 2025)
and Q1 2026 6-K filings — both names refer to the same asset.
Source: [Novo Nordisk Q4/FY2025 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353278/000035327826000006/caq42025.htm) (filed 2026-02-03); [Wikipedia, Amycretin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amycretin).

**Mechanism — correcting the working assumption.** This is **not** a
triple-agonist or oral-small-molecule GLP-1 program in the mold of Lilly's
retatrutide/orforglipron. Per Novo Nordisk's own Phase 2 publications,
zenagamtide is a **unimolecular dual GLP-1 and amylin receptor agonist** —
two covalently linked peptide analogs (one GLP-1-like, one amylin-like) in a
single molecule, dosed either subcutaneously (once weekly) or orally (once
daily, using an absorption enhancer). It is mechanistically closer to Novo's
own cagrilintide/CagriSema amylin-agonist family than to Lilly's OA assets,
though the strategic logic (weight loss + possible direct joint effects) is
similar.
Sources: [Mora P, et al., Lancet 2026 (SC Phase 2)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532080/), PMID 42532080; [Mora P, et al., Lancet 2026 (oral Phase 2)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532079/), PMID 42532079; [Wikipedia, Amycretin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amycretin).

**Program structure.** Zenagamtide's Phase 3 program is branded "AMAZE" and
spans multiple indications launched together starting Q1 2026: AMAZE 1 & 2
(obesity; obesity + T2D — the flagship 84-week trials), AMAZE 3 & 4 (obesity
+ obstructive sleep apnea), **AMAZE 5 & 6 (obesity + knee osteoarthritis —
our asset of interest)**, and AMAZE 12 (weight-loss maintenance). AMAZE 8
(head-to-head vs. semaglutide, T2D) and a separate cardiovascular-outcomes
trial (HF-POLARIS, heart failure + obesity) were added in Q2 2026. Knee OA
is one of six-plus parallel Phase 3 populations, not the flagship
indication.
Source: [Novo Nordisk Q1 2026 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353278/000035327826000018/caq12026.htm) (filed 2026-05-06); [Novo Nordisk Q2 2026 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353278/000035327826000023/caq22026.htm) (filed 2026-08-04).

**Trial design — AMAZE 5 (NCT07481630):**
- Phase 3, randomized, quadruple-blind (participant/care provider/
  investigator/outcomes assessor), parallel-group.
- 3 arms: NNC0487-0111 dose level 1, dose level 2, and placebo — a
  dose-ranging design.
- Once-weekly subcutaneous injection via PDS290 pre-filled pen, as adjunct
  to reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity.
- Population: obesity/overweight + knee OA, clinically diagnosed per ACR
  criteria with radiographic Kellgren-Lawrence grade 2–3 in the target knee.
- Estimated enrollment: 400. Actual start: 2026-04-13. Estimated primary
  completion / study completion: 2028-08-14.
- Primary endpoints: relative % change in body weight and change in WOMAC
  pain (NRS 3.1, 24-hour) score, both baseline (week 0) to week 80.
- ~82 sites across US, Canada, Australia, Denmark, Bulgaria, Greece, Italy,
  Poland, Spain, and Turkey. No independent data monitoring committee
  (`oversightHasDmc: false`).
Source: [ClinicalTrials.gov API record, NCT07481630](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07481630).

**Trial design — AMAZE 6 (NCT07509307):** Same Phase 3, quadruple-blind
design, but simpler: 2 arms (single NNC0487-0111 dose vs. placebo). Same
population, same primary endpoints (% body weight change, WOMAC pain score,
both to week 80), same estimated enrollment (400) and same estimated
completion date (2028-08-14). Actual start: 2026-04-20. ~81 sites in the
same countries as AMAZE 5 plus Puerto Rico.
Source: [ClinicalTrials.gov API record, NCT07509307](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07509307).

**Adjacent approved-drug angle: semaglutide.** Novo Nordisk has already
directly tested an approved GLP-1 drug (semaglutide 2.4 mg, the Wegovy dose)
in knee OA, in the completed **STEP 9** trial (NCT05064735), which read out
positively in the *New England Journal of Medicine* in October 2024 — well
before AMAZE 5/6 existed. STEP 9 is almost certainly the internal evidence
base that justified committing a second, purpose-built molecule
(zenagamtide) to a dedicated knee-OA indication. See Key Scientific
Literature and "Did" sections below.

## Timeline of Public Statements ("Said")

  Phase 2 topline results for **amycretin** (subcutaneous and oral) in type
  2 diabetes: up to 14.5% weight loss (SC) / 10.1% (oral) vs. ~2.5–2.6%
  placebo at 36 weeks, plus significant HbA1c reductions (up to 89.1% of
  patients reaching HbA1c <7%). States plans to **initiate a Phase 3

## Actual Outcomes ("Did")

**This is a very early-stage program. There is essentially no "did" history
yet for NNC0487-0111/zenagamtide in osteoarthritis specifically** — both
trials are still recruiting and neither has reached even its primary
completion date (2028-08-14). What follows is what has actually happened as
of this research pass (2026-08-21), plus the closest available read-through
evidence (a different Novo Nordisk molecule, semaglutide, tested in the same
indication).

- **AMAZE 5 (NCT07481630):** Status = **RECRUITING**, last verified by the
  sponsor 2026-07. Trial started as planned on 2026-04-13. No results,
  interim readouts, protocol amendments, delays, or safety signals found in
  any source reviewed.
  Source: [ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07481630](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07481630).

- **AMAZE 6 (NCT07509307):** Status = **RECRUITING**, last update posted
  2026-08-17 (the most recent record update of any source used in this
  profile). Trial started as planned on 2026-04-20. No results, interim
  readouts, protocol amendments, delays, or safety signals found.
  Source: [ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07509307](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07509307).

- **Parent-molecule Phase 2 (T2D, not OA) has fully read out and published:**
  the subcutaneous and oral zenagamtide Phase 2 dose-finding trials in type 2
  diabetes were published in *The Lancet* (2026-07-30/08-15 issue),
  confirming the positive November 2025 topline (statistically significant
  HbA1c reduction and weight loss, safety profile "consistent with incretin
  and amylin-based therapies"). This is real completed evidence for the
  molecule's mechanism and tolerability, but it is **not** OA-specific
  evidence.
  Sources: [Mora P, et al., Lancet 2026 (SC)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532080/), PMID 42532080; [Mora P, et al., Lancet 2026 (oral)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532079/), PMID 42532079.

- **The closest actual "did" data point for the GLP-1-in-OA thesis comes from
  a different Novo Nordisk drug, semaglutide, not from NNC0487-0111.** The
  Phase 3 **STEP 9** trial (NCT05064735) already completed and published:
  407 participants with obesity + moderate knee OA, 68 weeks, semaglutide
  2.4 mg vs. placebo (2:1). Results: body weight change −13.7% vs. −3.2%
  (p<0.001); WOMAC pain score change −41.7 vs. −27.5 points (p<0.001);
  SF-36 physical-function score improved more with semaglutide (+12.0 vs.
  +6.5 points, p<0.001). Discontinuation due to adverse events: 6.7%
  (semaglutide) vs. 3.0% (placebo), mostly GI. This is the real precedent
  underpinning Novo's decision to run a dedicated knee-OA Phase 3 program
  with its newer dual agonist.
  Source: [Bliddal H, et al., N Engl J Med 2024;391(17):1573-1583](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39476339/), PMID 39476339.

## Discrepancies / Watch Items

This is an early-stage program to watch rather than one with resolved
discrepancies yet. Notable items:

- **20-F silence, likely just timing.** The FY2025 20-F (filed 2026-02-04)
  contains zero mentions of zenagamtide/amycretin/osteoarthritis, even
  though the Q4 2025 6-K filed the day before it clearly discussed the
  parent molecule (T2D Phase 2 results). The OA indication itself wasn't
  disclosed anywhere until AMAZE 5 posted to ClinicalTrials.gov five weeks
  later (2026-03-19). Flag this for future company profiles in this
  project: **a foreign private issuer's 20-F can visibly lag its own more
  current 6-K disclosures by weeks**, so don't rely on the 20-F alone as
  the most current "said" source.

- **No dedicated company press release identified for AMAZE 5 or AMAZE 6.**
  Unlike the November 2025 Phase 2 T2D announcement (which had a proper
  GlobeNewswire release and a company news-details page), the OA-specific
  trial initiations appear to have been disclosed only via the routine
  ClinicalTrials.gov postings and a single grouped bullet in the Q1 2026
  6-K — no distinct press release with executive commentary was located.
  Worth re-checking periodically in case Novo Nordisk issues a dedicated
  announcement (e.g., at a future J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference or R&D
  Day presentation).

- **OA is a "one line among several" indication, not a flagship.** The
  zenagamtide Phase 3 program spans obesity, obesity+T2D, obesity+sleep
  apnea, obesity+weight-maintenance, obesity+knee OA, T2D-vs-semaglutide,
  and heart failure — at least 8 distinct Phase 3/3a populations. AMAZE 5/6
  get noticeably less individual company commentary than the flagship
  AMAZE 1/2 (obesity) or the new AMAZE 8/HF-POLARIS trials highlighted in
  Q2 2026. Watch whether OA retains priority if flagship obesity readouts
  (expected ~H1 2028 based on related trial timelines) disappoint, or if
  resourcing shifts.

- **Design asymmetry between the two "identical" trials.** AMAZE 5 is a
  3-arm dose-finding design (two dose levels + placebo) while AMAZE 6 is a
  2-arm fixed-dose design (one dose + placebo) — yet both share the same
  estimated primary completion date (2028-08-14) despite AMAZE 6 starting a
  week later. This is consistent with a coordinated dual-trial regulatory
  filing strategy (as Novo Nordisk used with the STEP trials), but worth
  monitoring for any date/design harmonization changes in future updates.

- **No independent DMC.** ClinicalTrials.gov lists `oversightHasDmc: false`
  for AMAZE 5 — standard for many obesity/metabolic trials but worth noting
  given the joint-safety concerns that have dogged other OA mechanisms
  (e.g., anti-NGF drugs like tanezumab, which required intensive imaging
  surveillance after joint-safety signals). No joint-safety monitoring
  detail specific to AMAZE 5/6 was found in the sources reviewed.

- **Milestones to track going forward:**
  - Whether Q3/Q4 2026 and subsequent quarterly 6-Ks give any AMAZE 5/6-
    specific update (enrollment status, dose selection, any protocol
    amendment).
  - Whether ClinicalTrials.gov status changes from RECRUITING (enrollment
    completion, early stopping, "why_stopped" field).
  - Whether Novo Nordisk explicitly cites STEP 9 semaglutide-OA data as
    scientific rationale in future investor materials — it would be a
    natural narrative link that hasn't yet appeared in the filings reviewed.
  - Competitive landscape: Eli Lilly's retatrutide/orforglipron OA angle,
    and unrelated third-party programs such as the intra-articular GLP-1
    analog reported to have received FDA Fast Track designation for knee OA
    in a Healio May 2026 article — a different company/molecule, not
    verified against Novo's program, but a signal the OA-via-GLP-1 space is
    becoming competitive.
