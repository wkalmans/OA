# Belief BioMed (Beijing) Co., Ltd — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** BBM-A101
**Mechanism:** AAV-vectored gene therapy delivering a customized human anti-inflammatory protein for continuous intra-articular expression, aiming to reduce inflammation and slow knee OA progression.

## Current Status
Belief BioMed (Shanghai-based, despite the "Beijing" entity name in our pull) received NMPA IND clearance in China for BBM-A101 gene therapy for knee OA. A Phase 1/2 trial (NCT07198555) began October 2025 evaluating safety, tolerability, and efficacy in moderate knee OA, with estimated primary completion December 2027; the company's own materials describe an earlier investigator-initiated trial with "promising" early safety/efficacy signals, though no detailed data have been published. The company has an existing gene-therapy partnership with Takeda China (unrelated to this OA asset).
