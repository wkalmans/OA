# Immunis, Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** IMM01-STEM
**Mechanism:** A "secretome" product — regenerative molecules secreted by partially differentiated pluripotent stem cells — targeting muscle atrophy (sarcopenia) associated with knee OA, rather than the joint itself.

## Current Status
Immunis, working with UCI Health/UCI Alpha Clinic, completed a Phase 1/2a trial of IMM01-STEM in elderly knee OA patients with muscle atrophy: participants showed increased 6-minute walk distance, clinically meaningful gait-speed improvement, and no severe adverse events, with reported improvements in pain, stiffness, and physical function. Building on this, Immunis is designing a larger randomized Phase 2b trial (STEM-MYO) and has separately received FDA clearance for a related Phase 2 trial (STEM-META) in sarcopenic obesity. This is a genuinely positive, if early, efficacy signal for a small company.
