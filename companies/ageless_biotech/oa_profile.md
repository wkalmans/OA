# Ageless Biotech, Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** OA-SYS
**Mechanism:** Autologous, minimally-manipulated adipose-derived stem cells delivered via Stromal Vascular Fraction (SVF) processing, injected intra-articularly to treat knee OA.

## Current Status
Ageless Biotech holds an FDA-accepted IND and is running an open-label Phase IIb trial (NCT06485843) evaluating OA-SYS safety/tolerability in moderate-to-severe knee OA, which started recruiting May 2025 with an estimated primary completion date of March 2028. Company press material (PRNewswire, May 2022) touts the FDA IND acceptance as a milestone, but no efficacy results, funding rounds, or partnerships have been publicly reported since.
