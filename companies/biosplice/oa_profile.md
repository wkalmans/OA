# Biosplice Therapeutics — Lorecivivint (SM04690) OA Profile

**Company:** Biosplice Therapeutics, Inc. (San Diego, CA) — formerly **Samumed, LLC** (renamed April 2021). Privately held; no SEC filing history (confirmed — see `company_profile.md`). Founded 2008 by Osman Kibar. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosplice_Therapeutics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosplice_Therapeutics)

**Asset:** Lorecivivint (development code **SM04690**; internal trial-code prefix **"OA-##"**), an intra-articular small-molecule CLK2/DYRK1A kinase inhibitor that modulates the Wnt signaling pathway and inflammatory pathways via alternative splicing.

Last updated: 2026-08-21 (research cutoff for this profile).

---

## Asset Overview

- **Mechanism:** Lorecivivint is injected directly into the knee joint (0.07 mg per 2 mL, 1–2x/year). It inhibits DYRK1A (anti-inflammatory effect → near-term pain/function relief) and selectively inhibits CLK2 and DYRK1A to downregulate Wnt pathway signaling (→ reduced cartilage breakdown) and is proposed to drive mesenchymal stem cell differentiation into chondrocytes (→ cartilage protection/regeneration). [biosplice.com/our-programs/detail.aspx?id=20](https://www.biosplice.com/our-programs/detail.aspx?id=20)
- **Why it's notable in the DMOAD field:** It is one of the furthest-advanced disease-modifying OA drug (DMOAD) candidates targeting both symptom relief (pain/function) *and* structural modification (joint space width) in a field where, as of 2026, "there are no intra-articular drugs that can restore the cartilage matrix" on the market. [PMID 41371377](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41371377/) GlobalData benchmarks Phase III OA drugs at only a 13% phase-transition success rate into Pre-Registration, underscoring how difficult this indication is. [pharmaceutical-technology.com](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/data-insights/lorecivivint-biosplice-therapeutics-osteoarthritis-likelihood-of-approval/)
- **Development history in brief:** Phase 1 (2014–15) → Phase 2a "OA-02" (2015–17) → Phase 2b "OA-04" (2017–18, positive) → positive End-of-Phase 2 FDA meeting (Feb 2019) → Phase 3 program launched (May 2019) → two pivotal Phase 3 trials "OA-10"/"OA-11" both **missed** their primary endpoint (announced Nov 2022) → a third, redesigned Phase 3 trial "OA-21" targeting an earlier-disease subgroup **also missed** its primary endpoint (announced Apr 2024) → NDA submitted to FDA anchored on long-term-extension trial "OA-07" data (submitted late 2025 / announced Jan 6, 2026). As of this writing lorecivivint is **not FDA-approved**; the NDA is pending. [biosplice.com NDA press release via BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/biosplice-announces-the-submission-of-its-new-drug-application-nda-to-the-fda-for-lorecivivint-lor-to-treat-knee-osteoarthritis)

## Actual Outcomes ("Did")

- **2015–2017 (OA-02 / NCT02536833, Phase 2a):** Completed. Positive structural signal (medial JSW) in a pre-specified subgroup. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02536833](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02536833)
- **2017–2018 (OA-04 / NCT03122860, Phase 2b):** Completed. Met primary endpoints per company; this is the trial the pivotal Phase 3 program was modeled on. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03122860](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03122860)
- **2019–2020 (NCT03706521, Phase 2 MRI sub-study):** **Terminated** — early termination "due to COVID-19 pandemic-related issues (e.g., temporary site closures)"; no formal statistical analysis performed. Not an efficacy failure. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03706521](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03706521)
- **2018–2021 (NCT03727022, Phase 2 bone-health study):** Completed, no negative signal reported. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03727022](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03727022)
- **2019–2021 ("OA-11" / NCT03928184, "STRIDES-Xray," Phase 3, 56-week):** Completed. **Primary endpoint (Pain NRS at week 12) NOT met**: LOR −2.24 vs. placebo −2.49 (p=0.185). No discernible treatment effect on any secondary endpoint; neither arm showed meaningful medial-JSW loss over 52 weeks (so the structural comparison was also uninformative — floor effect). [PMID 39808286 / PubMed abstract via NCBI eutils](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39808286/) — trial registration confirmed in [PMID 39495154](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39495154/)
- **2020–2021 ("OA-10" / NCT04385303, "STRIDES-1," Phase 3, 28-week):** Completed. **Primary endpoint (Pain NRS at week 12) NOT met** in the 498-patient full analysis set; no other efficacy endpoint reached significance. A *post-hoc* subgroup (KL Grade 2 patients) showed nominal significance at week 4 only. [PMID 39808288](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39808288/)
- **Nov 18, 2022** — STAT News reports on the failures: "Biosplice, once the most valuable biotech startup, sees a pair of osteoarthritis trials fail." [statnews.com/2022/11/18](https://www.statnews.com/2022/11/18/biosplice-once-the-most-valuable-biotech-startup-sees-a-pair-of-osteoarthritis-trials-fail/)
- **Jul–Dec 2021 (NCT04931667, 3-year open-label Phase 3):** **Terminated** after only ~5 months (started 2021-07-15, terminated 2021-12-13) — "terminated by Sponsor based on a business decision not due to safety." Timing coincides almost exactly with Biosplice's Dec 9, 2021 layoff notice to California's EDD and its Aug–Nov 2021 discontinuation of the hair-loss (dalosirvat) program — consistent with a broader cost-cutting retrenchment rather than a trial-specific problem. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04931667](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04931667); layoff timing per [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/biosplice-lays-off-41-drops-baldness-program-as-12b-valuation-confuses-everyone) and [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2022/02/08/biosplice-san-diego-biotech-mass-layoffs/)

## Discrepancies / Watch Items

1. **"Terminated" ≠ "failed" for OA-07 (NCT04520607).** This is the specific contradiction the tracker flagged. OA-07 shows status **TERMINATED** on ClinicalTrials.gov, but its own `why_stopped` field says it was closed administratively on schedule ("no safety concerns") — and it went on to generate the positive long-term structural/pain data (Nov 2023, Apr 2024 press releases) that anchors the 2026 NDA. **Do not read "TERMINATED" as "the drug failed here."** Meanwhile the two trials that actually **did fail on their stated primary endpoint** — OA-10/STRIDES-1 (NCT04385303) and OA-11/STRIDES-Xray (NCT03928184) — both carry the more reassuring-sounding status **COMPLETED**. The ClinicalTrials.gov status field alone is not a reliable proxy for trial success/failure for this program; the `why_stopped` text and the peer-reviewed outcome papers (PMID 39808286, 39808288) are the ground truth.

2. **Cost-cutting-driven termination, not efficacy-driven.** NCT04931667 (3-year open-label extension) was killed after only 5 months, in the same window as Biosplice's Dec 2021 layoff notice and the axing of its hair-loss program — this looks like it was swept up in a broader financial retrenchment rather than a response to bad OA data (the OA-10/OA-11 failure readouts weren't disclosed until nearly a year later, Nov 2022).

3. **Three consecutive Phase 3 primary-endpoint misses (OA-10, OA-11, OA-21), one NDA.** The core "said vs. did" gap: Biosplice's public narrative since 2018 has consistently framed each new dataset as "successful" or "positive," but the pivotal, pre-specified primary endpoints across three separate Phase 3 trials designed to support approval were **not met** in their full analysis populations. The NDA instead leans on (a) a long-term open-label/blinded-extension trial (OA-07) with an unusual crossover design, and (b) post-hoc subgroup analyses (KL2/early KL3 patients) that were not the trials' original pre-specified primary populations. Whether FDA views this evidence package as sufficient for approval — given three ITT-population misses — is the key open question; no regulatory designation (Fast Track/Breakthrough) or PDUFA date has been disclosed.

4. **Independent literature is more cautious than the company's own framing, right at NDA-filing time.** A January 2026 independent meta-analysis (6 RCTs, 2,179 patients; PMID 42402776) found only modest WOMAC pain improvement at weeks 12/24, with **no significant improvement in function, Pain NRS, patient global assessment, or structural outcomes**, and no consistent dose-response — a notably more skeptical read than Biosplice's press releases issued the same month.

5. **Trial-count mismatch — dataset likely incomplete.** Biosplice's Jan 2026 NDA release cites "11 total clinical trials." This project's `clinicaltrials_oa_trials_raw.json` keyword pull found only **10** lorecivivint/SM04690/Biosplice/Samumed trials (not the "9" originally estimated — worth correcting that assumption). At least one additional trial is known but wasn't captured by the keyword pull: **NCT02951026**, a 60-month observational extension of the Phase 2a/2b trials (published 2025, PMID 39755925), likely missed because its title doesn't contain "lorecivivint" or "SM04690." Recommend adding NCT02951026 to the raw dataset and re-checking the pull script's matching logic for observational/extension study titles.

6. **Heliyon meta-analysis retraction (literature-reliability caveat, not a drug-safety finding).** An earlier (2023) independently-authored meta-analysis of lorecivivint (Heliyon, PMID 37576256) was retracted in Feb 2025 (PMID 40201252) — but this was part of a mass retraction wave at Heliyon following an Elsevier-wide integrity audit (authorship-qualification concerns), unrelated to Biosplice's own trial data. Treat pre-2025 Heliyon-sourced meta-analyses on this asset with caution but don't read the retraction itself as a finding against lorecivivint.

7. **No FDA regulatory milestones (Fast Track, Breakthrough, RMAT) found in any source** despite an 11-year, 10+ trial, 1,800+ patient program — worth periodically re-checking as the NDA moves through review.
