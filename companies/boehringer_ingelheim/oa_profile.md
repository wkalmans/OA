# Boehringer Ingelheim — Osteoarthritis Profile

*Lighter-touch pull (web/PubMed only — Boehringer Ingelheim is privately held, no SEC filings). Compiled 2026-08-21.*

## Asset Overview

**Mobic (meloxicam)** — oral NSAID (COX-2 preferential), approved and long marketed for OA and RA pain. This is a mature, off-patent branded product, not a pipeline asset. The Mobic brand has been discontinued in the U.S.; generic meloxicam remains widely available there, and Boehringer continues to market Mobic-branded meloxicam in other territories (e.g., Singapore, Malaysia product labels currently posted on Boehringer's own site). [Drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/mobic.html), [Boehringer SG label (PDF)](https://content.boehringer-ingelheim.com/DAM/504057f7-63f4-44e1-bf81-aee600f7603e/mobic-sg-pi.pdf)

**BI 1026706** — an oral **bradykinin B1 receptor (B1R) antagonist**. B1R signaling is induced by inflammation/tissue injury and drives pro-inflammatory cell activation/migration, so blocking it was hypothesized to have anti-inflammatory/analgesic effects. [ResearchGate PDF summary](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/340306693_Bradykinin_1_Receptor_Antagonist_BI1026706_Does_Not_Reduce_Central_Retinal_Thickness_in_Center-Involved_Diabetic_Macular_Edema), [AdisInsight](https://adisinsight.springer.com/drugs/800037435)

## Current Status

- **BI 1026706 in OA: discontinued / inactive.** The only OA-related trial, [NCT02126826](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02126826) (Phase 1, multiple rising oral doses in healthy subjects and knee-OA patients — OA patients were used as a convenience/PK-PD population, not for an efficacy readout), ran May–October 2014 and is listed **Completed**, record last verified December 2018. No further OA-specific trials of this compound have been registered since. (Source: ClinicalTrials.gov API record for NCT02126826, fetched 2026-08-21.)
- Boehringer subsequently tested BI 1026706 in **two other indications**, both of which read out negative:
  - **Diabetic macular edema (DME):** did not reduce central retinal thickness vs. placebo. [PubMed 32818112](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32818112/)
  - **COPD/pulmonary inflammation** (segmental LPS/endotoxin challenge in smokers): not superior to placebo, no evidence of anti-inflammatory effect. [PubMed 37562641](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37562641/)
  - No BI 1026706 trials of any kind have been registered since the 2023 COPD publication; the compound appears to have been shelved across all indications, OA included.
- **No other current OA pipeline candidate found.** Boehringer's public "Human Pharma Clinical Pipeline" page and its stated R&D therapeutic areas (immunology/respiratory, cardio-renal-metabolic, CNS, oncology, eye health) do not list osteoarthritis, arthritis, or musculoskeletal disease as an active area as of this pull. [Boehringer Clinical Pipeline](https://www.boehringer-ingelheim.com/science-innovation/human-health-innovation/clinical-pipeline) — note: this page renders via JavaScript and could not be fully scraped by our fetch tool; confirmed instead via multiple web searches on BI's stated 2025–2026 pipeline priorities, none of which mention OA. Treat the "no OA pipeline" conclusion as reasonably confident but not a line-by-line page audit.
