Methodology. Status is read from SEC filings (10-K/10-Q/20-F/40-F/6-K/8-K), ClinicalTrials.gov trial records, PubMed literature, and company websites/press releases — not from a company's own framing of its program. Companies marked “deep dive” were checked directly against primary SEC filing text; others were checked against public web and literature sources. Findings marked as inferred are based on absence of evidence ― e.g., an asset quietly dropped from a pipeline page ― rather than an explicit company confirmation.
