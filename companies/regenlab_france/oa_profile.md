# RegenLab France SAS — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Regen Matrix-PRP-XLHA (RM-PRP-XLHA, delivered via the RegenMatrix medical device)
**Mechanism:** Single intra-articular injection combining platelet-rich plasma (PRP) with cross-linked hyaluronic acid (XLHA), intended to reduce pain and improve joint function/quality of life in moderate-to-severe knee OA.

## Current Status
RegenLab (Switzerland/France, established 2003 as a PRP/cell-therapy device maker) is running a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, three-arm trial (NCT05727371) comparing a single RM-PRP-XLHA injection against Hylan G-F 20 (a standard HA viscosupplement) and placebo in moderate-to-severe knee OA, evaluating pain reduction, quality of life, and gait analysis in a subset of patients. This is a device/procedure trial (Phase not applicable) rather than a drug program; RegenLab already markets related PRP-HA combination products commercially, so this appears to be a life-cycle/label-expansion study rather than an early pipeline bet. No trial results were found yet.
