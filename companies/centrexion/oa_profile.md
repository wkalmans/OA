# Centrexion Therapeutics — CNTX-4975 (OA Profile)

*Lighter-touch pull — last researched 2026-08-21. Centrexion is privately held; no SEC filings exist, so this relies on company materials, press releases, ClinicalTrials.gov, and PubMed.*

## Asset Overview

- **CNTX-4975** (also called CNTX-4975-05) is a synthetic, ultra-pure trans-capsaicin formulation ("STRATI" — Synthetic TRans cApsaicin ulTra-pure Injection), given as an intra-articular injection into the knee.
- **Mechanism:** Activates and then selectively de-functionalizes local TRPV1-expressing pain fibers in the joint, aiming to give long-lasting (potentially up to 6 months) analgesia from a single (or repeat) injection without systemic opioid or NSAID exposure.
- **Differentiation:** Non-opioid, non-disease-modifying, purely a pain-fiber–targeted local analgesic — mechanistically distinct from NGF-pathway drugs (tanezumab/fasinumab/LEVI-04) or gene therapies (GNSC-001, PCRX-201). FDA granted Fast Track designation in January 2018 for knee OA pain. [Source](https://www.biospace.com/centrexion-therapeutics-announces-fast-track-designation-granted-by-fda-to-cntx-4975-for-treatment-of-knee-osteoarthritis-pain)
- **Stage:** Two pivotal Phase 3 trials (VICTORY-1/OAKP-301, VICTORY-2/OAKP-304) are complete with posted results; a third, open-label Phase 3 (VICTORY-3) also completed. No later-stage or new trials have started since.

## Current Status (as of most recent verifiable information, 2023–2024)

**Both pivotal Phase 3 trials missed their individual primary endpoints.** Per ClinicalTrials.gov posted results for VICTORY-1 (NCT03429049, single-injection, n=307 ITT): least-squares mean difference vs. placebo in pain-with-walking (NPRS) at Week 12 was -0.33 (95% CI -0.84 to 0.17), **p=0.19 — not significant**, though a secondary endpoint (WOMAC pain) reached significance (p=0.026). [ClinicalTrials.gov results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03429049)

This matches contemporaneous reporting: Endpoints News reported in 2021 that "Centrexion's knee pain drug fails two PhIII studies, but ex-Pfizer CEO Kindler's biotech might still ask for FDA nod" — the two nearly-identical trials individually missed but showed numerical superiority over placebo, and the company (then led by ex-Pfizer CEO Jeffrey Kindler) planned to pool the two datasets and consider both a peer-reviewed publication and a potential FDA submission. [Source](https://endpoints.news/exclusive-centrexions-knee-pain-drug-fails-two-phiii-studies-but-ex-pfizer-ceo-kindlers-biotech-might-still-ask-for-fda-nod/)

That pooled/integrated analysis (combining OAKP-301 and OAKP-304) was presented at ACR Convergence 2023 and reportedly showed statistically significant results across the pooled Week-12 endpoints (NPRS p=0.030; WOMAC A p=0.005; WOMAC B p=0.004; WOMAC C p=0.006; PGIC p=0.004), with no difference in radiographic OA progression or rapidly-progressive OA versus placebo through Week 52. [ACR abstract](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/combined-datasets-for-cntx-4975-osteoarthritis-knee-pain-oakp-phase-3-trials-oakp-301-and-304-to-assess-overall-efficacy-and-safety-of-an-intra-articular-injection-iai-of-cntx-4975-05-cntx-in/)

**I could not verify any confirmed FDA submission, licensing deal, or discontinuation announcement after the 2023 ACR presentation.** The company's own pipeline page (centrexion.com/pipeline) still lists CNTX-4975 generically as "Phase III" but gives no updated trial status, and no press releases, clinical trial registrations, or news coverage were found describing an NDA filing, a partner, or a decision to shelve the program. A January 2024 GlobeNewswire item titled "Market Overview of Centrexion Therapeutics' CNTX-4975 Drug, 2023–2032" is syndicated market-research content, not company news, and should not be read as a status update. The most concrete post-2023 data point is a small ~$3.26M debt financing round reported for Centrexion around January 2024 (per third-party funding trackers; not independently confirmed via a press release). **Bottom line: the program appears stalled in a "post-pivotal-trial-miss, exploring options" state rather than actively advancing, discontinued, or licensed out** — but I could not find primary-source confirmation of its status after early 2024, so treat this as an open question for the next deep dive.
