# Nature Cell Co. Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** JointStem
**Mechanism:** Autologous adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cell therapy, intra-articular injection for knee osteoarthritis.

## Current Status
JointStem has the longest and deepest trial history of any company in this batch, running (under sponsors R-Bio and then Nature Cell Co. Ltd.) from an early Phase 1/2 study in 2008 through Phase 2 trials (2015-2018) to a completed Phase 3 trial in 2020 (NCT03990805), with a current Phase 2b/3a study (NCT04368806) listed active-not-recruiting through 2027 and multiple long-term follow-up studies extending to 2027-2029. Nature Cell's own site references a January 2025 press release about "FDA IND Approval," suggesting the company has cleared a step toward US clinical development in addition to its long-running Korean trial program. No confirmation of Korean MFDS conditional/full marketing approval was found in this check — despite the extensive trial history and a completed Phase 3, JointStem's Korean regulatory approval status appears unresolved, consistent with the drug's long-reported history of approval delays.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- JointStem became the first therapy from a Korean company to receive FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation; Nature Cell submitted a BT meeting request on March 18, 2026, with a formal FDA meeting scheduled for May 2026 to define the U.S. accelerated-approval pathway, Phase 3 trial design, and endpoints. <b>FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation secured; accelerated-approval pathway meeting set for May 2026.</b> https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/biotechnology/nature-cell%e2%80%99s-jointstem-becomes-the-first-korean-company-to-acquire-breakthro-1003919
- Nature Cell is also pursuing a Nasdaq listing via ADR, with a listing application expected between September and November 2026. https://firstwordpharma.com/story/7148715
