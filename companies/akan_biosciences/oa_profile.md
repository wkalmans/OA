# Akan Biosciences, LLC — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** StroMel
**Mechanism:** Autologous adipose-derived stem cell therapy, positioned as anti-inflammatory and reparative for knee osteoarthritis.

## Current Status
StroMel is registered as a Phase 1/2 safety and tolerability trial in moderate-to-severe knee OA (NCT04750252, sponsor Akan Biosciences, LLC), listed as not-yet-recruiting on ClinicalTrials.gov. The company's own site (akanbio.com) describes StroMel as already "in Phase 2 clinical trial for osteoarthritis (OA) of the knee" and outlines plans to expand into other joints, wound healing, Crohn's disease, sports injuries, and even autism — a broad, early-stage regenerative-medicine platform typical of a small stem-cell company. No funding, partnership, or efficacy data were found beyond the company's own site and the trial registration.
