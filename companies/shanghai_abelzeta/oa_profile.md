# Shanghai AbelZeta Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Allogeneic adipose-derived mesenchymal progenitor cell therapy — now developed as Lotazadromcel (brand name AlloJoin)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic human adipose-derived mesenchymal progenitor cells (haMPCs) injected intra-articularly to suppress joint inflammation and promote cartilage repair, aiming for disease modification rather than symptom relief alone.

## Current Status
This program originated at AbelZeta Pharma/Shanghai AbelZeta but was spun off in 2023 into ElpasBio, which now leads its development as Lotazadromcel (AlloJoin). ElpasBio completed patient enrollment (500+ patients) in a Phase III pivotal trial in China in May 2026, with a data readout targeted for 2027 and a planned NDA submission to China's CDE. ElpasBio also announced a commercialization partnership with Fosun Kairos to market AlloJoin in Mainland China, Hong Kong, and Macau if approved — a genuinely notable development for a program that started in a smaller originator company. The goal is to be the first regenerative-medicine product to demonstrate disease modification in knee OA, though it is not yet approved.
