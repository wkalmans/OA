# Levicept — LEVI-04 (OA Profile)

*Lighter-touch pull — last researched 2026-08-21. Levicept is privately held (UK); no SEC filings exist, so this relies on company materials, press releases, ClinicalTrials.gov, and PubMed.*

## Asset Overview

- **LEVI-04** (p75NTR-Fc) is a fusion protein of the p75 neurotrophin receptor (p75NTR) ectodomain, given intravenously (monthly dosing in the completed Phase 2 trial).
- **Mechanism:** Acts as a decoy/supplementation of the endogenous p75NTR, inhibiting **neurotrophin-3 (NT-3)** signaling — an indirect, receptor-decoy approach to the neurotrophin pathway, as opposed to the direct anti-NGF monoclonal antibody approach used by tanezumab and fasinumab.
- **Differentiation:** Company and independent presenters (EULAR 2026) have directly compared LEVI-04's joint-safety profile to anti-NGF therapies (fasinumab), arguing the NT-3/p75NTR mechanism may avoid the rapidly-progressive osteoarthritis (RPOA) and joint-safety signals that limited anti-NGF antibody development. In the completed Phase 2 trial, LEVI-04 showed **no increased incidence of joint pathologies, including RPOA**, versus placebo. [Lancet, PMID 41905364]
- **Potential disease modification:** Post-hoc MRI analysis found significant, dose-dependent reductions in bone marrow lesions (BML) — a marker of OA structural disease — that correlated with symptom improvement, which the company describes as a first for a molecule in this class. [BioSpace, EULAR 2026](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/levicept-to-present-data-suggesting-levi-04-may-modify-disease-in-osteoarthritis-at-eular-2026)
- **Stage:** Phase 2 complete and published; FDA has accepted an IND application (Jan 2026) enabling the next stage of US clinical development.

## Current Status (as of 2026-07, most recent verifiable information)

- The pivotal Phase 2 trial (NCT05618782 / EudraCT 2021-006540-28) is **COMPLETED** (completion date 2024-05-20 per ClinicalTrials.gov) and its full results were **published in The Lancet on 2026-03-28** (funded entirely by Levicept). [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05618782) | [Lancet](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(26)00131-5/fulltext)
- Trial enrolled 518 participants (knee OA, WOMAC pain ≥4/10, radiographic OA) randomized to placebo or LEVI-04 at 0.3, 1.0, or 2.0 mg/kg monthly through week 16. **Primary endpoint (WOMAC pain change vs. placebo at week 17) was met at all three doses**, with the highest dose (2.0 mg/kg) showing the largest effect: LS mean difference -0.79 (95% CI -1.24 to -0.35), p=0.0024, effect size 0.43. No increase in serious adverse events, treatment-emergent adverse events, or joint pathology (including RPOA) versus placebo. [PMID 41905364 abstract, via PubMed]
- FDA **accepted Levicept's IND application for LEVI-04 on 2026-01-20**, explicitly framing it as "a potentially disease-modifying treatment for osteoarthritis," enabling the company to proceed with further clinical development in a broader patient population and longer-term dosing studies. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/20/3221340/0/en/Levicept-Announces-FDA-Acceptance-of-IND-Application-for-LEVI-04-a-Potentially-Disease-Modifying-Treatment-for-Osteoarthritis.html)
- Company has been actively building out its executive team in 2026 (Chief Development Officer in February; Chief Financial Officer with significant public-markets/Nasdaq-listing experience in July), which is consistent with preparing for a larger financing round, a pivotal-stage program, or a potential public listing — though no financing round or IPO has been announced as of this pull.
