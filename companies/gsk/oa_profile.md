# GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) — Osteoarthritis Pipeline Profile

*Light-touch pull, compiled 2026-08-21. Sources: company website/pipeline reports, press releases, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed. Not a deep-dive SEC review.*

## Bottom line

GSK does **not currently have an active osteoarthritis drug program**. Its most recent OA candidate, **GSK3858279** (anti-CCL17 antibody), had its Phase 2 trial terminated for futility in 2024. Its two other historical OA-adjacent candidates — **otilimab (GSK3196165)** and **relacatib** — were both discontinued years ago, and a fourth candidate flagged in our data, **GW842166**, was also abandoned after disappointing Phase 2 knee OA results. GSK's official pipeline documents (Q1/Q2 2026) organize the company around respiratory/immunology, oncology, HIV, and infectious disease — osteoarthritis is not a listed focus area. [GSK pipeline page](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/research-and-development/our-pipeline/)

## Asset Overview

### GSK3858279 (anti-CCL17 monoclonal antibody)
- Novel, first-in-class human monoclonal antibody that functionally inhibits the chemokine CCL17, a mediator of inflammatory pain. Mechanistically distinct from NGF- or cytokine-targeted OA pain drugs. [PubMed 39419538](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39419538/)
- This is the asset our ClinicalTrials.gov pull found listed as "Phase 1/2" — confirmed correct; it progressed from a Phase 1 proof-of-concept study (NCT03485365) into a Phase 2 dose-finding study, "MARS-17" (NCT05838742).

### GSK3196165 / otilimab (anti-GM-CSF monoclonal antibody)
- Confirmed: otilimab is indeed GSK3196165, a human monoclonal antibody against granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF). [Lancet Rheumatology](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanrhe/article/PIIS2665-9913(20)30229-0/abstract)
- GSK's primary development effort for otilimab was in **rheumatoid arthritis** (Phase 3 ContRAst program), not OA. A separate, smaller Phase 2a trial tested it in **inflammatory hand osteoarthritis** (NCT02683785).

### Relacatib (SB-462795)
- A cathepsin K inhibitor, originally developed for postmenopausal osteoporosis with osteoarthritis also examined as a potential indication. [ScienceDirect topic overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/relacatib)
- Confirmed old and discontinued: trials were halted after Phase I due to drug-drug interactions with commonly co-prescribed drugs (acetaminophen, ibuprofen, atorvastatin), and it showed poor selectivity versus other cathepsins. GSK abandoned it around 2007. [Science.org](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/cathepsin-k-promising-target-fades-out)

### GW842166 (CB2 receptor agonist)
- A pyrimidine cannabinoid-2 (CB2) receptor agonist tested for inflammatory/OA pain. Showed analgesic signal in preclinical models and in acute post-dental-extraction pain, but this did not translate to efficacy in knee OA patients at 100 mg and 350 mg once-daily doses over 4 weeks (NCT00479427, NCT00447486). [ScienceDirect topic overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/gw-842166); [NCATS Inxight Drugs](https://drugs.ncats.io/drug/VL1I6P2DZ8)
- Program was withdrawn; no further development found.

## Current Status (as of most recent data found)

| Asset | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GSK3858279 | **Terminated** — Phase 2 "MARS-17" trial (NCT05838742) stopped early in Dec 2024 after meeting pre-planned futility criteria; sponsor said the decision was not for safety reasons. Results posted Nov 2025 showed no clear separation from placebo on knee pain. | [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05838742](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05838742) |

## Recent News

- **Nov 10, 2025** — ClinicalTrials.gov results posting confirms MARS-17 (GSK3858279 Phase 2, NCT05838742) was terminated after an interim analysis of ~120 participants who completed their Week 12 visit found the drug "was not as effective as intended." [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05838742](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05838742); [HRA study summary](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/mars-17-study-in-adults-with-pain-due-to-osteoarthritis-of-the-knee/)
- **Mar 28, 2025** — Phase 1 GSK3858279 data published in *Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases*, showing the drug had beaten placebo on knee OA pain in the earlier, smaller Phase 1 proof-of-concept study — a positive result that preceded the Phase 2 failure. [PubMed 39419538](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39419538/)
- **Oct 2022** — GSK terminates the otilimab RA Phase 3 program (ContRAst) after Phase 3 futility; this effectively ended active company interest in otilimab as a platform, including any residual OA interest. [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-dumps-major-pipeline-prospect-otilimab-lackluster-phase-3-data-rheumatoid-arthritis)

*No dated GSK press release specifically announcing the OA program terminations was found; the above is reconstructed from ClinicalTrials.gov status records and third-party trade press, since these were relatively minor pipeline items GSK did not headline in its own newsroom.*
