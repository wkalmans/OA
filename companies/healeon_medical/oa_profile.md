# Healeon Medical Inc — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** PRP Concentrate product (biocellular regenerative therapy)
**Mechanism:** Autologous adipose-derived stem/stromal cells combined with HD-PRP (platelet-rich plasma), delivered as a minimally invasive injection for OA.

## Current Status
Healeon Medical is sponsor of an active, recruiting trial evaluating adipose-derived biocellular regenerative therapy (own stem/stromal cells plus HD-PRP) for osteoarthritis (NCT04238143, phase not applicable). The Healeon Medical web presence now redirects to Bimini Health Tech, which markets the "Healeon HD PRP" and "Healeon DUET" PRP-preparation systems — though those product pages emphasize bone-graft handling applications rather than OA specifically, and don't specify FDA clearance details. This reads as a small orthobiologics device company running an investigator-style registry/study rather than a drug-development program with disclosed efficacy data.
