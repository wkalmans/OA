# CellSeed Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** CLS2901C (human allogeneic chondrocyte sheets)
**Mechanism:** Temperature-responsive cell-sheet engineering; allogeneic chondrocyte sheets (sourced from polydactyly-derived cartilage tissue) transplanted onto damaged cartilage combined with high tibial osteotomy and marrow stimulation to regenerate joint surface.

## Current Status
CellSeed (Japan) is running a Phase III study of CLS2901C in knee osteoarthritis (NCT06549686), comparing an osteotomy + cell-sheet transplantation arm against osteotomy alone, seeking approval under Japan's PMD Act. The platform builds on over a decade of academic research (Tokai University, Dr. Masato Sato) with prior autologous chondrocyte-sheet studies showing confirmed safety/efficacy signals since 2011, giving this program a more mature evidence base than most on this list. No Phase III results are out yet.
