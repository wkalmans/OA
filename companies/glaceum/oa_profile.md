# Glaceum — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Vutiglabridin (formerly HSG 4112)
**Mechanism:** Orally available small-molecule pyranochromenyl phenol derivative; described as an inflammation-mediator inhibitor.

## Current Status
Glaceum (Korea) is running a Phase 2a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of once-daily oral vutiglabridin over 26 weeks in knee osteoarthritis patients (NCT06845553), following completed Phase 1 safety/PK work in healthy subjects. Vutiglabridin is also being developed by Glaceum for obesity/NASH indications, with OA as one of several pursued indications. No efficacy results specific to OA have been reported yet.
