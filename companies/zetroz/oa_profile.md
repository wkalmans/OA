# ZetrOZ, Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** sam device (Sustained Acoustic Medicine) + 2.5% Diclofenac Ultrasound Coupling Patch
**Mechanism:** Wearable long-duration therapeutic ultrasound device combined with a diclofenac topical patch; the ultrasound is intended to improve local blood flow/soft-tissue healing while the diclofenac patch delivers topical NSAID pain relief for knee OA.

## Current Status
ZetrOZ Systems' sam device is FDA-cleared for home use (soft-tissue healing/joint circulation) and is already commercially marketed with press coverage describing its use for knee OA and low-back pain relief in "aging athletes." A trial (NCT04391842) combines the device with a diclofenac ultrasound coupling patch for knee OA; this reads as a device/consumer-health company layering a pharma combination study on an already-marketed product rather than a pure drug-development play. No published efficacy results for the combination trial were found beyond the registration.
