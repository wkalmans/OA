# DuoGenic StemCells Corporation (DGSC) — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Low-dose PCP-PBMCs injection
**Mechanism:** Autologous peripheral blood mononuclear cell (PBMC) intra-articular injection for knee osteoarthritis.

## Current Status
DuoGenic StemCells Corporation is sponsor of record for a Phase 2/3 trial evaluating intra-articular PBMC injection for knee OA (NCT07703280), currently listed as not-yet-recruiting. No corporate website could be located (duogenicstemcells.com does not resolve), and no press coverage, funding news, or additional pipeline information was found.
