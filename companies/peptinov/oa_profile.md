# Peptinov SAS — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** PPV-06
**Mechanism:** Active immunotherapy ("kinoid"/anti-cytokine vaccine) that induces the patient's own antibodies against interleukin-6 (IL-6), an inflammatory mediator implicated in OA.

## Current Status
Peptinov, a French biotech, completed a Phase 1 dose-escalation study of PPV-06 assessing safety and immune response (NCT04447898). It has since registered a Phase 2 trial, "PPV-06 Vaccine in Inflammatory Knee Osteoarthritis" (NCT06605963, not yet recruiting), testing six subcutaneous injections over two years in patients with an inflammatory OA phenotype. The vaccine-style anti-IL-6 approach is a distinctive mechanism versus typical OA injectables/cell therapies. The company website could not be reached during this check, so no funding or partnership details beyond the trial registrations were found.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- Peptinov published positive Phase 1 results for PPV-06 in Nature Communications (Dec 2025): the randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 24 patients with inflammatory knee OA showed an excellent safety/tolerability profile, with no serious adverse events or dose-limiting toxicities. <b>Positive Phase 1 data published; Phase 2 program (204 patients, 7 European countries) planned for 2026.</b> https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/peptinov-publishes-positive-phase-1-results-for-ppv-06-in-nature-communications-302646257.html
- The company is preparing a broader Phase 2 clinical program for 2026, including a multicenter trial across seven European countries enrolling 204 patients with inflammatory knee osteoarthritis. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41193416/
