# Genascence Corporation — GNSC-001 (OA Profile)

*Lighter-touch pull — last researched 2026-08-21. Genascence is privately held; no SEC filings exist, so this relies on company materials, press releases, ClinicalTrials.gov, and PubMed.*

## Asset Overview

- **GNSC-001** is a recombinant AAV (self-complementary rAAV2.5) gene therapy encoding human interleukin-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra), delivered as a **single intra-articular injection** into the osteoarthritic knee.
- **Mechanism:** Aims to provide long-term, sustained local inhibition of IL-1 signaling — a cytokine pathway implicated in cartilage breakdown and OA pain/inflammation — potentially offering disease-relevant, not just symptomatic, benefit from one injection instead of chronic dosing.
- **Differentiation:** Positioned as a potential **first-in-class gene therapy for knee OA**; distinct from Pacira BioSciences' competing IL-1Ra gene therapy candidate **PCRX-201** (different vector platform — high-capacity adenovirus, not AAV — acquired by Pacira from GQ Bio Therapeutics). The two are not affiliated. [Pacira PCRX-201](https://www.pacira.com/innovation/pcrx-201/)
- Technology originated from academic work at Mayo Clinic, University of Florida, and NYU Langone Health (researchers Christopher Evans, Steven Ghivizzani, Paul Robbins), licensed into Genascence, founded 2017, Palo Alto, CA. [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/genascence-receives-11-6-million-award-from-california-institute-for-regenerative-medicine-to-help-advance-its-lead-gene-therapy-program-in-knee-osteoarthritis-301754759.html)
- **Stage:** Phase 1b (sponsor-run, dose-ranging) complete; Phase 2b/3 planned to start in 2026.

## Current Status (as of 2025-07 / most recent verifiable information)

- Lead trial **DONATELLO** (Phase 1b, NCT05835895) — double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging study of GNSC-001 (10^12 or 10^13 vector genomes, ± oral steroid immune-conditioning) vs. placebo in 67 patients across 10 US centers — is **ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING** per ClinicalTrials.gov, last updated 2025-08-01, with an estimated study completion of 2029-05 (long-term follow-up continues even though dosing/primary analysis is done). [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05835895)
- **12-month primary endpoint met (announced 2025-05-12):** GNSC-001 was safe and well-tolerated across all dosing arms through 12 months, with no treatment-related deaths, serious adverse events, or withdrawals; the key secondary endpoint (sustained IL-1Ra expression in synovial fluid reaching/maintaining therapeutic target levels) was also met, with steroid pre-treatment associated with higher, more durable expression. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/genascence-phase-1b-donatello-trial-evaluating-potential-first-in-class-gene-therapy-for-knee-osteoarthritis-oa-meets-primary-endpoint-showing-gnsc-001-was-safe-and-well-tolerated-across-multiple-dosing-arms)
- FDA granted **Fast Track designation** in Q4 2024, and **Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation** on 2025-07-16 — RMAT is meant to enable closer FDA collaboration to accelerate late-stage development. [PackGene](https://www.packgene.com/frontier/071625-genascence/)
- Genascence has reportedly completed a successful FDA meeting on Phase 2b/3 trial design and **plans to initiate the Phase 2b/3 efficacy study in 2026** — but as of this pull (2026-08-21) I could not find a press release confirming the trial has actually started or that a first patient has been dosed. Treat "2026 initiation" as company guidance, not a confirmed event.

## Key Scientific Literature

- De la Vega RE, Sellon JL, Smith J, et al. (incl. Genascence co-authors Keravala A, Chalberg TW). "A phase 1 clinical trial shows safe, sustained, AAV-mediated expression of IL-1Ra in the human osteoarthritic knee joint." *Science Translational Medicine* 2025;17(801):eadu9804. PMID: [40465688](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40465688/). This is the foundational Mayo Clinic/University of Florida-led Phase 1 trial (9 patients, sc-rAAV2.5-IL-1Ra, three dose levels) underlying the GNSC-001 program: no serious vector-related adverse events, sustained IL-1Ra elevation in synovial fluid through 1 year, and improvement in baseline pain/function scores during the study. Note: this appears to be the earlier investigator-sponsored trial that established proof-of-concept, distinct from (but scientifically continuous with) Genascence's own sponsor-run DONATELLO Phase 1b trial, which does not yet appear to have a full peer-reviewed publication (press-release data only as of this pull).
- Evans CH, Ghivizzani SC, Robbins PD. "Osteoarthritis Gene Therapy: A 30-Year Journey From Concept to Clinical Trials." *Journal of Orthopaedic Research* 2026;44(2):e70141. PMID: [41607103](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41607103/) — review by the Genascence-affiliated academic founders covering the field's history including their own IL-1Ra work.
- Evans CH, Ghivizzani SC, Robbins PD. "The 2024 OREF Clinical Research Award: Progress Toward a Gene Therapy for Arthritis." *J Am Acad Orthop Surg* 2024;32(23):1052-1060. PMID: [39284030](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39284030/).
- For context/comparison (not Genascence's asset): Cohen S, Hochberg MC, et al. "Safety, biodistribution, and exploratory clinical outcomes of a novel intra-articular IL-1Ra gene therapy (PCRX-201) in moderate-to-severe knee osteoarthritis: 2-year results of a phase 1 study." *Ann Rheum Dis* 2026. PMID: [42552219](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42552219/) — this is Pacira's competing (non-AAV) IL-1Ra gene therapy, included here only to avoid confusion between the two programs.
