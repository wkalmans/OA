# Novartis — LNA043 (Osteoarthritis) — Said vs. Did Profile

**Asset:** LNA043 (no INN/brand name ever assigned — listed as "TBD" in Novartis's own pipeline tables)
**Status as of this writing (Aug 2026): development discontinued.** The headline "promising Phase 2 DMOAD" framing commonly attached to this program needs a caveat — see Discrepancies section. The positive data is real but is from Phase 1 / an early Phase 2a proof-of-concept in a narrower population; the pivotal Phase 2b trial in knee OA (ONWARDS) failed on both structural and pain endpoints and the program was discontinued in early 2025.

---

## Asset Overview

- **Mechanism:** A derivative/agonist of angiopoietin-like 3 (ANGPTL3). Binds the fibronectin receptor integrin α5β1 on mesenchymal stem cells and chondrocytes, inducing chondrogenesis and cartilage matrix synthesis; in a post-hoc transcriptomics analysis, a single injection reversed the OA gene-expression signature in cartilage for at least 21 days. [Gerwin et al., Nat Med 2022, PMID 36456835]
- **Origin:** Discovered internally at the Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research (NIBR, Basel/San Diego/Cambridge) via a phenotypic screen of human MSCs. All authors on the founding Nat Med 2022 paper are Novartis/NIBR-affiliated. **No evidence of external in-licensing was found** — despite this research task's initial premise that LNA043 may have started life under a different sponsor's development code, nothing in the SEC filings, PubMed literature, or press coverage reviewed here supports that; it appears to be a wholly in-house Novartis discovery. Flagging this so it isn't repeated as fact elsewhere in the tracker.
- **Route:** Intra-articular injection.
- **Novartis's own therapeutic-area classification:** "Immunology" in its pipeline tables (not orthopedics/musculoskeletal) — worth noting for anyone filtering Novartis disclosures by TA.
- **Development stage entered "current phase" (Phase II):** 2021, per Novartis 20-F pipeline tables.
- **Regulatory designation:** FDA Fast Track for knee osteoarthritis, granted ~September 2021.
- **Outcome:** Program discontinued following the Phase 2b (ONWARDS) readout, disclosed in Novartis's Q4/FY2024 results (filed Jan 31, 2025). No longer appears in the FY2025 20-F pipeline at all.

## Timeline of Public Statements ("Said")

- **2015-11** — First-in-human Phase 1 trial (NCT02491281) begins: single ascending-dose, IA LNA043 in 28 knee-OA patients scheduled for total knee replacement, 2h/7d/21d pre-surgery. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02491281]
- **2017-09** — Phase IIa proof-of-concept trial begins (NCT03275064), two-part design — Part A: focal articular cartilage lesions; Part B: knee OA — 142 patients total. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03275064]
- **2020-09** — Japan-specific Phase 1 PK/safety bridging study begins (NCT04564053). [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04564053]
- **2021-05-31** — Phase IIb "ONWARDS" trial begins (NCT04864392): 5-year, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 5 arms (four LNA043 dose/schedule regimens + placebo), 576 patients, primary endpoint = change in cartilage thickness (central medial tibiofemoral compartment) at Week 104. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04864392]
- **2021-08-27** — Companion Phase II trial begins (NCT04814368) testing IA canakinumab followed by IA LNA043 combination in knee OA, 23 patients. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04814368]
- **2021-09-02** — Novartis press release: FDA grants Fast Track designation to LNA043 for knee OA, calling it a "potential first in class disease modifying treatment for osteoarthritis" and stating the Phase IIb (ONWARDS) study "is underway." [https://www.novartis.com/news/novartis-receives-fda-fast-track-designation-lna043-osteoarthritis-knee]
- **2021-10-26** — Q3 2021 earnings 6-K press release exhibit highlights the Fast Track grant as a pipeline milestone of the quarter. [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036821000072/a211026-99_1.htm]
- **2022-09** — OARSI 2022 World Congress abstract reports Phase IIa (Part A, focal cartilage lesions) results: 4× weekly IA LNA043 20mg injections produced significantly less cartilage volume loss vs. placebo; no symptomatic (pain) benefit observed; most common AE was injection-site swelling, no treatment-related SAEs. [DOI 10.1016/j.joca.2022.02.276 — https://www.oarsijournal.com/article/S1063-4584(22)00310-7/fulltext, as summarized in Brandt et al. 2025, PMID 40002768]
- **2022-12-01** — Nature Medicine publishes Gerwin et al., the Phase 1 safety/PK/mechanism paper, explicitly stating LNA043 "is currently in a phase 2b trial (NCT04864392) in patients with knee OA." [PMID 36456835 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36456835/]
- **2023-02-01** — FY2022 20-F pipeline table: LNA043, knee osteoarthritis, Phase II, first planned regulatory submission "≥2026." [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036823000006/nvs-20221231.htm]
- **2024-01-31** — FY2023 20-F pipeline table: LNA043, osteoarthritis, Phase II, first planned submission now "≥2027" — a one-year slip from the prior year's disclosure. [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036824000004/nvs-20231231.htm]
- **2024-04-23 / 2024-07-18 / 2024-10-29** — Q1, H1 and Q3 2024 interim 6-K pipeline tables continue to list LNA043 as active Phase 2, submission "≥2027," FDA Fast Track noted; no red flags disclosed in the "News update" column through the Q3 2024 filing (Oct 29, 2024). [Q1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036824000017/a240423-99_2.htm | H1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036824000019/nvs-20240630-99_2.htm | Q3: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036824000022/a241029-99_2.htm]
- **Feb 2025 (search cutoff mid-2024)** — Brandt, Malone & Kean's DMOAD review describes ONWARDS as an active "two-part, 10-year study" testing 581 subjects at 76 sites in 15 countries, "expected to conclude in 2027" — reflecting the publicly registered trial plan as of the paper's mid-2024 literature search, before the discontinuation was disclosed. [PMID 40002768 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40002768/]

## Actual Outcomes ("Did")

- **2018-03-06** — Phase 1 (NCT02491281) completes. Primary safety endpoint met; short serum PK, confirmed cartilage penetration, no immunogenicity signal. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02491281; PMID 36456835]
- **2022-09-06** — Phase IIa (NCT03275064) completes, 142 patients enrolled (matched plan). Structural signal (less cartilage volume loss vs. placebo) seen in the focal-cartilage-lesion cohort; **no pain/symptomatic benefit** in that same cohort. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03275064; OARSI abstract DOI 10.1016/j.joca.2022.02.276]
- **2024-06-24** — Canakinumab+LNA043 combination trial (NCT04814368) completes/terminates with only 23 participants; ClinicalTrials.gov records the stop reason simply as **"Sponsor Decision"** — no further public detail found. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04814368]
- **2024-09-29** — ONWARDS (NCT04864392) reaches its Week 104 primary-endpoint analysis and is **terminated early**. ClinicalTrials.gov: *"Study early terminated due to lack of effect (did not meet primary/secondary endpoints). This decision was not linked to safety concerns."* Actual enrollment 576 (vs. 581 planned per the 2025 review paper). [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04864392]
  - **Primary endpoint** (Week 104 Δ cartilage thickness, central medial tibiofemoral compartment, mm) — all five arms lost cartilage at nearly identical rates, with no dose-response and no separation from placebo:
    - LNA043 40mg×3 Q6m: **−0.291**
    - LNA043 40mg×3 Q12m: **−0.303**
    - LNA043 20mg×3 Q6m: **−0.267**
    - LNA043 40mg×1 Q6m: **−0.257**
    - Placebo: **−0.267**
    (Posted results, ClinicalTrials.gov Results tab: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04864392)
  - **Key secondary endpoint** (Week 104 Δ WOMAC pain) — all arms improved by roughly −28 to −33 points, again with no separation from a large placebo response (−29.98). Same source.
- **2025-01-31** — Novartis's Q4/FY2024 results 6-K pipeline "News update" states: *"LNA043 Osteoarthritis 2 – Project discontinued following Ph2 readout."* Filed the same day as the FY2024 20-F, which lists LNA043 under "Removed — Development discontinued" alongside four other axed programs (Cosentyx-rotator cuff, CFZ533, JDQ443, QGE031, XXB750). [6-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036825000007/a250131-99_2.htm | 20-F: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000137036825000004/nvs-20241231.htm]
- **2025-05-29** — ONWARDS trial's formal "completion date" on ClinicalTrials.gov (final follow-up/data closeout), roughly four months after the discontinuation had already been disclosed to investors. Record last updated 2026-04-02. [https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04864392]
- **2026-02-04** — FY2025 20-F filed; **LNA043 does not appear anywhere in the document** (confirmed by searching the full filed text) — it has been fully dropped from Novartis's pipeline disclosure. [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114448/000111444826000004/nvs-20251231.htm]
- No peer-reviewed publication or conference presentation of the negative ONWARDS Phase 2b data was located as of this research (Aug 2026) — the raw numbers above come only from the results tab Novartis posted to ClinicalTrials.gov, not from a journal article.

## Discrepancies / Watch Items

1. **"Promising Phase 2 data" framing is imprecise.** The only positive, published efficacy signal (less cartilage volume loss vs. placebo) came from Phase 1/early Phase 2a work in a narrower population — patients with focal articular cartilage lesions, not general knee OA — and even there showed no pain benefit. The actual Phase 2b registrational-track trial in the broader knee-OA population (ONWARDS) failed on both the structural primary endpoint and the pain secondary endpoint. Anyone citing "LNA043 had promising Phase 2 data" should specify which Phase 2 study they mean.
2. **Slipping timeline preceded outright failure.** Novartis's own "first planned regulatory submission" year moved from "≥2026" (FY2022 20-F) to "≥2027" (FY2023 20-F) the year before the program was discontinued — a pattern worth watching for in other Novartis pipeline entries as an early warning sign.
3. **Quiet disclosure.** The discontinuation was announced only as a single line inside the routine Q4/FY2024 pipeline table ("Project discontinued following Ph2 readout"), not via a standalone press release or dedicated 6-K exhibit — no separate announcement was found in EDGAR full-text search or general web search. Track this pattern for other Novartis discontinuations too.
4. **Gap between disclosure and formal trial closeout.** Investors were told the project was discontinued as of Jan 31, 2025; the ClinicalTrials.gov record wasn't marked "completed" until May 29, 2025, and was still being updated as recently as April 2, 2026 — worth periodic rechecking for late-added detail or a possible negative-results manuscript.
5. **Pattern across Novartis's broader OA structural pipeline (lower confidence, partially unverified):** Novartis also terminated a second OA structural candidate around the same period — QUC398, Phase 2 (NCT05462990), stopped for "Sponsor Decision," completion dated 2025-04-08 — confirmed directly via ClinicalTrials.gov (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05462990). Trade press (Fierce Biotech headline "Novartis terminates phase 2 osteoarthritis trial testing ADAMTS-5 inhibitor") suggests QUC398 = M6495, an ADAMTS-5 nanobody in-licensed from Merck KGaA in 2020, stopped for "insufficient effect on pain relief" — **this identity and the reason-for-stop were not independently confirmed here** (the Fierce Biotech article was blocked by Cloudflare on direct fetch; content came only from a search-engine snippet synthesis). A third program, LRX712 (a chondrogenic drug, per the same press summary), was also reportedly discontinued for "insufficient effect on knee cartilage" — again **not independently verified** beyond the search snippet; our own ClinicalTrials.gov data shows LRX712's Phase 2 trial (NCT04097379) as "COMPLETED" rather than "TERMINATED," so the claimed discontinuation would have happened as a portfolio decision after trial completion, not as an early stop — this needs direct-source confirmation before being treated as fact. If both hold up, Novartis has now struck out on three separate OA structural/DMOAD mechanisms (ANGPTL3-agonist, ADAMTS-5 inhibition, and whatever LRX712's target is) within a few years of each other — a notable pattern for the "did" side of the OA tracker generally, not just for LNA043.
6. **No in-licensing found.** Contrary to the initial research brief's suggestion that LNA043 might have originated under a different sponsor's code name, all available evidence (Nat Med 2022 author affiliations, absence of any licensing-related SEC filing or press release) points to LNA043 being a purely in-house NIBR discovery. Correct this assumption in any other tracker entries that may repeat it.
