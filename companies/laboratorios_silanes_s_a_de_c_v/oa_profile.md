# Laboratorios Silanes S.A. de C.V. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Celecoxib + Acetaminophen fixed-dose combination
**Mechanism:** Combines a COX-2 selective NSAID (celecoxib) with acetaminophen for multi-mechanism analgesia in OA pain.

## Current Status
Laboratorios Silanes, a Mexican pharmaceutical company, is running the Phase 3 "PRECEDENT" trial (NCT06863636) comparing a celecoxib/acetaminophen fixed-dose combination against celecoxib alone for pain in acute exacerbation of osteoarthritis; the trial started September 2024 with an estimated primary completion of October 2025. Separately reported was a Phase 3 U.S. trial of a long-acting, once-weekly injectable celecoxib/acetaminophen formulation for chronic OA. No efficacy results or regulatory filing news were found beyond these trial descriptions.
