# TricelX Inc. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** XytriX (Mesenchymal Stem Cell Injection)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic umbilical-cord-tissue-derived mesenchymal stem cell (hUC-MSC) biotherapeutic injected intra-articularly for knee osteoarthritis.

## Current Status
TricelX filed an IND with the FDA for XytriX in March 2026 and concurrently requested Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation — one of the FDA's highest-priority development pathways — for Grade I-IV knee OA. The FDA has since issued a "Study May Proceed" letter, clearing the Phase 1/2 trial (NCT07487766, three dose cohorts: 10M/30M/50M cells) to begin. This is a genuinely positive recent regulatory milestone (IND clearance + RMAT request) for a small company, though no clinical efficacy data yet exists.
