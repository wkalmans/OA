# Peptinov SAS — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** PPV-06
**Mechanism:** Active immunotherapy ("kinoid"/anti-cytokine vaccine) that induces the patient's own antibodies against interleukin-6 (IL-6), an inflammatory mediator implicated in OA.

## Current Status
Peptinov, a French biotech, completed a Phase 1 dose-escalation study of PPV-06 assessing safety and immune response (NCT04447898). It has since registered a Phase 2 trial, "PPV-06 Vaccine in Inflammatory Knee Osteoarthritis" (NCT06605963, not yet recruiting), testing six subcutaneous injections over two years in patients with an inflammatory OA phenotype. The vaccine-style anti-IL-6 approach is a distinctive mechanism versus typical OA injectables/cell therapies. The company website could not be reached during this check, so no funding or partnership details beyond the trial registrations were found.
