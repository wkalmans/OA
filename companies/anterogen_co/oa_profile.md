# Anterogen Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** ANT-301
**Mechanism:** Adult stem cell (stem cell replacement) therapy injected into the joint to address cartilage/joint degeneration.

## Current Status
Anterogen is a South Korean bio-venture specializing in adult stem cell therapies and orphan drugs. ANT-301 is in an open-label, single-center, dose-escalation Phase 1 study evaluating efficacy and safety in patients with knee osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence Grade III/IV) (NCT06539429, study ID ANT-301-102), which started October 2024 and is listed as active but not recruiting, with estimated primary completion in May 2026. No results or partnership announcements were found beyond the trial registration and company's own promotional material.
