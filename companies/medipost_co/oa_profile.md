# Medipost Co Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** CARTISTEM (Phase 3), High Hyal Plus (Phase 2), SMUP-IA-01 (Phase 1/2)
**Mechanism:** CARTISTEM is an umbilical-cord-blood-derived allogeneic mesenchymal stem cell therapy for knee OA cartilage repair; High Hyal Plus and SMUP-IA-01 appear to be earlier-stage intra-articular biologic/cell-therapy candidates in Medipost's broader OA pipeline (no detailed mechanism disclosure found for these two beyond the ClinicalTrials.gov listings).

## Current Status
CARTISTEM is Medipost's lead and most advanced asset: already marketed in Korea since 2012, it met all primary and secondary endpoints (WOMAC and ICRS cartilage-repair grade) in a Japan Phase 3 trial, and the FDA has cleared a U.S. Phase 3 plan (randomized, double-blind, vs. surgical cartilage debridement, 60+ sites in the US/Canada, several hundred patients with Kellgren-Lawrence Grade 2-3 knee OA); the first U.S. patient was dosed July 2026. This is a genuinely strong, multi-region disease-modifying data package for a mid-size company. High Hyal Plus and SMUP-IA-01 were not covered in available press materials — no public disclosure was found for either beyond their ClinicalTrials.gov registrations.
