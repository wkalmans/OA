# Mather's Pharm. Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Laybon Tab. (also referenced as Layla Tab. / PG201)
**Mechanism:** Oral botanical extract (multi-plant-source formulation) with anti-inflammatory/chondroprotective activity, used as a plant-based alternative to NSAIDs for knee OA symptom relief.

## Current Status
Mather's Pharm. is running a Phase 4 study of Laybon Tab. in knee osteoarthritis patients (NCT07203547), estimated to start around December 2025 with primary completion in December 2027 — this is a post-marketing/confirmatory study, implying the product is already approved and sold. A related product, PG201 (brand name Layla), was shown in an earlier published Phase IV trial to significantly reduce knee pain and improve function comparably to SKI306X (Joins), suggesting Laybon may be part of the same or a related botanical drug family, though this is not confirmed as identical.
