# Bioventus LLC — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** PTP-001 (brand name MOTYS™)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic placental tissue particulate, delivered as a single intra-articular injection for knee OA.

## Current Status
Bioventus (NASDAQ: BVS) is an established public orthobiologics company best known for marketed hyaluronic-acid (HA) injections (DUROLANE, GELSYN-3, SUPARTZ FX) and the 2022 CartiHeal acquisition. PTP-001/MOTYS is a newer, non-HA pipeline asset that has progressed from a Phase 1 safety study (NCT04632966) through Phase 2 (NCT05100225, active, not recruiting) to a Phase 3 efficacy/safety trial (NCT07219771) that is now recruiting. Notably, the Phase 3 trial's listed sponsor is "Doron Therapeutics Inc." rather than Bioventus directly, suggesting the asset may have been spun out, licensed, or is being advanced through a related entity — worth flagging for follow-up but not confirmed via press release. No public efficacy data or regulatory milestones beyond trial registrations were found in this pulse-check.
