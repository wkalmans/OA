# Jiangsu Aosaikang Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** ASKC200 (topical liniment)
**Mechanism:** Novel-mechanism topical analgesic intended for sustained pain relief in osteoarthritis; specific molecular target not disclosed.

## Current Status
Jiangsu Aosaikang Pharmaceutical (ASK Pharm), an established Chinese pharma company, is running a multi-center, randomized, double-blind Phase 1/2 trial of ASKC200 liniment (5% vs. 1% concentration, applied over four consecutive days) in knee OA patients to determine safety, PK, and the Phase 2 recommended dose (NCT06614608). China's NMPA accepted the New Drug Clinical Trial Application for ASKC200 Liniment in April 2023. No efficacy results have been reported yet; the company itself is a larger established generics/specialty pharma player, but this specific OA asset is early-stage.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- The original Phase 1/2 dose-finding trial (NCT06614608) is now listed as Active, Not Recruiting: it started September 12, 2025, reached primary completion April 28, 2026, with estimated full completion September 30, 2026. A second, separate ASKC200 trial, "ASKC200 for Knee Osteoarthritis Pain" (NCT07742852), has also been registered — a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, active-drug/placebo-controlled efficacy and safety study — indicating the program has advanced beyond the initial dose-finding stage. (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06614608, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07742852)
