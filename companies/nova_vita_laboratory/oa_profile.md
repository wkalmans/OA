# Nova Vita Laboratory — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** NANOVAE (acellular allogeneic human amniotic fluid)
**Mechanism:** Filtered human amniotic fluid rich in growth factors, cytokines, and extracellular matrix molecules (e.g., hyaluronic acid, collagen substrates), injected intra-articularly for tissue protection/repair.

## Current Status
Nova Vita Laboratory is running a Phase 1/2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of intra-articular NANOVAE in knee osteoarthritis (NCT06606561), enrolling 24 subjects across an open-label lead-in group (single 2ml dose) and a randomized, placebo-controlled group (two 2ml doses), measuring WOMAC scores, range of motion, and serum/synovial biomarkers. No results, funding, or partnership news were found beyond the trial registration and the company's own website description of the science.
