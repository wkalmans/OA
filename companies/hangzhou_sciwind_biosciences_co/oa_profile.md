# Hangzhou Sciwind Biosciences Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Ecnoglutide Injection (XW003)
**Mechanism:** Long-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist (once-weekly), approved in China for obesity/weight management and type 2 diabetes; any OA benefit would be indirect, via weight loss reducing mechanical joint load — the same rationale behind Lilly's tirzepatide and Novo Nordisk's semaglutide programs in knee OA that are already in our tracked-company set.

## Current Status
Ecnoglutide is a commercially approved GLP-1 analog in China for obesity and T2D; our search found no OA-specific trial data, results, or company statements connecting Ecnoglutide to osteoarthritis beyond its presence in the ClinicalTrials.gov pull. Given the mechanism, this is almost certainly a weight-loss-mediated knee OA study analogous to Lilly/Novo Nordisk's GLP-1 OA programs, but no public disclosure was found confirming trial design, phase details specific to OA, or results.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- A dedicated Phase 3 OA trial has now been registered and confirms the previously-suspected program: a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III study (NCT07734311) evaluating ecnoglutide injection in obese participants with knee osteoarthritis (KOA) started August 3, 2026, and is currently recruiting, with an estimated primary completion date of May 31, 2028. This resolves the prior "no OA data found" status. (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07734311)
