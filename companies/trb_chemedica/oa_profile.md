# TRB Chemedica — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Artrodar (diacerein-based product)
**Mechanism:** Diacerein, an interleukin-1 (IL-1) inhibitor, positioned as a potential disease-modifying (structure-slowing) agent for OA in addition to symptom relief.

## Current Status
TRB Chemedica (Switzerland) markets diacerein under the Artrodar brand and is sponsor of an ongoing Phase 3 trial evaluating diacerein's structure-modifying effect in knee OA via MRI cartilage-volume measurement (NCT04318041, recruiting). A prior completed Phase 3 trial (NCT02688400) compared diacerein against celecoxib on both symptoms (6 months) and structural change (2 years, MRI). Diacerein is a long-established, older anti-inflammatory compound already marketed in various international markets (not FDA-approved in the US, historically due to GI/liver tolerability concerns), so this reads as an established player running incremental structure-modification trials on an existing product rather than a novel pipeline asset. No new efficacy results were found beyond the trial registrations.
