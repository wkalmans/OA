# Paradigm Biopharmaceuticals Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Injectable Pentosan Polysulfate Sodium (iPPS), proposed brand Zilosul
**Mechanism:** Subcutaneously injected semisynthetic glycosaminoglycan; anti-inflammatory (NF-κB inhibition), analgesic (NGF suppression in osteocytes), and chondroprotective (inhibits cartilage-degrading enzymes) — positioned as disease-modifying, not just symptomatic.

## Current Status
Paradigm is an ASX-listed company with real public disclosure. Its Phase IIb knee OA trial met key secondary endpoints, showing improved knee function, pain reduction, and reduced bone marrow lesion grade/volume/area, with six-month data suggesting the drug may slow disease progression — a disease-modification signal rare in this space. The company has since partnered with City St George's, University of London (announced 2026) to further study the drug's pain mechanism, and continues to pursue a Phase 3 (CLIP study) path toward eventual FDA filing.
