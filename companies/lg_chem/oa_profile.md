# LG Chem — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Synovian® (hyaluronic acid injection, HMDA cross-linked)
**Mechanism:** Cross-linked hyaluronic acid viscosupplement, single/short-course intra-articular injection for knee OA symptom relief.

## Current Status
LG Chem is a major Korean conglomerate, and Synovian is a real, already-marketed HA injectable for knee OA in South Korea — not an early pipeline candidate, which explains its "NA phase" listing on ClinicalTrials.gov. Rather than running new efficacy trials of its own, LG Chem completed a post-market observational study on Synovian injection-site reactions (NCT04369261). More tellingly, Synovian has become the market's reference standard: multiple competitors (Shin Poong Pharmaceutical's SP5M001/SP5M002, Yooyoung Pharmaceutical's YYD302, DALIM TISSEN's CartiPRO) have run head-to-head non-inferiority trials against it. This indicates Synovian is an established, commercially significant HA product for LG Chem's life-sciences unit rather than an active R&D program — no new clinical or regulatory news was found.
