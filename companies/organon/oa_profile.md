# Organon — OA Pipeline Profile (Light-Touch Pull)

*Pulled 2026-08-21. Web + PubMed sources only; no SEC filing deep-dive this pass.*

## Bottom Line

**Organon has no active osteoarthritis drug development program.** Everything found in this pull is legacy Merck data (pre-2021 spinoff) related to **etoricoxib** (brand name Arcoxia), an older NSAID/COX-2 inhibitor, plus a handful of very old comparator trials. Organon's current public-facing pipeline and R&D communications make **zero mention of osteoarthritis** as a therapeutic area. This is not a data gap in our records — it's a genuine absence of current OA activity at the company.

## Asset Overview

- No novel OA drug candidate identified for Organon, past or present, beyond the inherited legacy Merck compound **etoricoxib** (Arcoxia), a COX-2-selective NSAID.
- Etoricoxib is a mature, largely off-patent/generic-adjacent molecule, not an active pipeline asset. It was never FDA-approved for the U.S. market (Merck received a non-approvable letter in April 2007 over cardiovascular safety concerns) though it is approved and marketed in other countries. ([Drugs.com — Arcoxia](https://www.drugs.com/arcoxia.html))
- Organon's OA-related clinical trial history (as sponsor of record for legacy Merck studies) consists of older etoricoxib trials, e.g.:
  - NCT00757627 — "Patient-Reported Effectiveness and Safety of Etoricoxib in Osteoarthritis"
  - NCT01980940 — topical etoricoxib gel formulation PK/efficacy study
  - NCT01429168, NCT01554163, NCT00242489, NCT00250445 — earlier etoricoxib-vs-comparator OA trials
  
  (all found via ClinicalTrials.gov search; https://clinicaltrials.gov)
