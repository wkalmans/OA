# Shandong Suncadia Medicine Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** HRS-2129 Tablet
**Mechanism:** Not explicitly disclosed in public sources; being developed as an oral analgesic for moderate-to-severe knee OA pain (also studied for postoperative orthopedic analgesia and, separately, gout flare pain), suggesting a pain/inflammation-pathway small molecule rather than a disease-modifying agent.

## Current Status
Shandong Suncadia is a Chinese pharmaceutical company (affiliated with Hengrui/Suncadia group). HRS-2129 is in a Phase Ib dose-finding study (NCT07351968, ~200 participants, 1 site, recruiting) evaluating efficacy, safety, and population pharmacokinetics in knee OA pain. The same compound is also being advanced into a separate Phase 2 gout-flare-pain trial, indicating the company is exploring it as a broader analgesic across pain indications. No results or partnership news were found beyond the trial registrations.
