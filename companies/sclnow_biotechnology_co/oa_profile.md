# Sclnow Biotechnology Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Umbilical cord mesenchymal stem cell therapy (19#iSCLife®-OA)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic human umbilical-cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (hUC-MSCs) injected intra-articularly to address cartilage defect and joint degeneration.

## Current Status
Sclnow Biotechnology is developing an allogeneic hUC-MSC therapy for osteoarthritis, tested in trial NCT03383081 (recruitment status listed as of December 2025), aiming to verify efficacy/safety and establish dosing across different degrees of cartilage defect. Third-party pipeline trackers list the asset as Phase 2 for knee OA, though our ClinicalTrials.gov pull spans Phase 1-3 listings for the unnamed proprietary product line. No results, funding, or partnership news were found beyond the trial registration.
