# Nuwacell Biotechnologies Co., Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** NCR100
**Mechanism:** Induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived mesenchymal stromal cells (iMSCs) delivered by injection for tissue repair and immunomodulation in knee OA.

## Current Status
Nuwacell is a Chinese cell-therapy company; NCR100 is notable as reportedly the first iPSC-derived MSC product approved for clinical trials in China. Multiple trials are active, including a safety/efficacy study in knee OA and a separate biodistribution study using zirconium-89-labeled NCR100 to track cell trafficking after injection — a relatively sophisticated translational touch for a small company. No efficacy results or partnership/funding news were found beyond the trial registrations.
