# Eli Lilly — Osteoarthritis Pipeline Profile

*Compiled 2026-08-21. Sources: SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K exhibits fetched directly from sec.gov), PubMed/eutils, Lilly investor press releases, ClinicalTrials.gov (via repo's `data/clinicaltrials_oa_trials_raw.json`), trade press.*

## Bottom line

Lilly has **three active Phase 3 osteoarthritis programs and one Phase 2 program**, and they are **not the same kind of drug**. Retatrutide, orforglipron, and eloralintide are all GLP-1-family (GIP/glucagon/amylin) **obesity drugs** being tested in **overweight/obese knee-OA patients**, where the working hypothesis Lilly has put on the record is weight-loss-driven mechanical unloading of the joint — every one of these trials enrolls only overweight/obese OA patients and treats percent body-weight change as a co-primary or joint-registration endpoint alongside WOMAC pain. This confirms the task's framing for those three assets. **LY4065967 breaks that framing**: it is not a GLP-1/amylin/weight-loss drug at all. It's an oral small molecule in Lilly's long-running, unrelated "Chronic Pain Master Protocol" (CPMP) platform, tested directly for OA pain (and separately for diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain) with no weight/obesity eligibility criterion. It is also the only one of the four assets **never mentioned by name in any Lilly SEC filing** reviewed for this pull.

Retatrutide's OA program (TRIUMPH-4) is the most advanced and the most publicly promoted: it read out positive topline Phase 3 results on Dec 11, 2025, and Lilly's Q2 2026 earnings release says the OA indication is now bundled into a single obesity/OSA/OA Biologics License Application it plans to file with the FDA in Q1 2027.

## Asset Overview

### Retatrutide (LY3437943) — GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist
- Mechanism: activates GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. [PubMed PMID 41090431](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41090431/)
- OA rationale (per Lilly-authored trial-design paper): tested as a treatment for obesity and "two related complications" — obstructive sleep apnea and knee osteoarthritis — via a basket-trial design (TRIUMPH-1/2/3/4) that recruits participants with shared obesity-driven disease exposures. Co-primary endpoints are percent body-weight change and WOMAC pain subscale change; the mechanism-of-benefit hypothesis is weight loss, not a claimed direct joint/cartilage effect. [PubMed PMID 41090431](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41090431/)
- Stage: Phase 3. Standalone OA trial **TRIUMPH-4** (NCT05931367) is COMPLETED with positive topline data (Dec 2025); the wider obesity registration package (TRIUMPH-1/2/3) also completed through 2026. Not yet approved for any indication. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05931367](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05931367)

### Orforglipron (LY3502970, branded Foundayo™) — oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist
- Mechanism: non-peptide, orally administered GLP-1 receptor agonist. [PubMed PMID 42479349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42479349/)
- OA rationale: same weight-loss/obesity-complication logic as retatrutide — the OA trial is titled "...Participants With Obesity or Overweight and Osteoarthritis (OA) of the Knee" and requires the overweight/obesity comorbidity for enrollment. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07153471](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07153471)
- Stage: Approved (April 2026, U.S., for obesity) — but the OA indication is still Phase 3, unapproved. OA trial "ATTAIN-OA PAIN" (NCT07153471) started Sept 15, 2025, ~800 participants, ~74-week trial, primary completion expected April 2028. [PubMed PMID 42479349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42479349/); [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07153471](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07153471)

### Eloralintide (LY3841136) — selective amylin receptor agonist
- Mechanism: once-weekly injectable, selective amylin receptor agonist (not a GLP-1 agonist — different receptor class, same "incretin-adjacent" obesity-drug family). [Lilly SEC 10-K FY2025, pipeline table]
- OA rationale: same pattern as the other two — trial enrolls "Participants With Osteoarthritis Knee Pain and Obesity or Overweight." [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07353931](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07353931)
- Stage: Phase 3, least mature of the three obesity-class OA programs. "ENLIGHTEN-4" (NCT07353931) started Feb 9, 2026; primary completion not expected until March 2028. No efficacy data yet. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07353931](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07353931)

### LY4065967 — oral analgesic candidate, mechanism NOT the obesity/GLP-1 class
- **Correction to the assumed framing**: this is not a weight-loss drug being repurposed for OA. It is an oral small molecule tested through Lilly's "Chronic Pain Master Protocol" (CPMP) — the same standing platform Lilly has used for years to test a rotating slate of analgesic candidates in OA pain (our repo's own ClinicalTrials data shows this same CPMP framework previously used for LY3016859, LY3526318, LY3556050, LY3857210, and earlier LY2951742/LY2828360 — most now COMPLETED without any evident Phase 3 advancement, and LY2951742 TERMINATED). LY4065967's OA trial has no weight/obesity eligibility requirement. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07641517](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07641517)
- Mechanism: **not independently confirmed from a primary source.** One secondary aggregator (Synapse/PatSnap-style compound tracker, surfaced via web search, not a Lilly document, ClinicalTrials.gov field, or PubMed record) describes it as an AT2R (angiotensin II type 2 receptor) antagonist. Treat this as **unverified** until a primary source is found.
- Stage: Phase 2. Two known indications in development: osteoarthritis pain ("OA07," NCT07641517, started June 25, 2026, primary completion April 2027) and diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain (NCT07285018). Earliest human studies are ADME/PK work (radiolabeled [14C]-LY4065967 study NCT07039045; healthy Japanese-participant study NCT06594159). [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07641517](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07641517)
- **Not mentioned in any of the six SEC filings reviewed** (10-K FY2024, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q Q3 2025, 10-Q Q2 2026, Q4 2025 earnings PR, Q2 2026 earnings PR) — searched directly for "4065967," "LY4065967," "chronic pain master protocol," and "CPMP" in each; zero hits in all six. At Phase 2 it is evidently below Lilly's disclosure threshold for the named-compound pipeline table.

## Actual Outcomes ("Did")

- **TRIUMPH-4 (retatrutide, NCT05931367)** — COMPLETED. 445 participants, 68 weeks, randomized 1:1:1 to retatrutide 9mg / 12mg / placebo. WOMAC pain subscale: −4.5 pts (−75.8%) on 9mg, −4.4 pts (−74.3%) on 12mg, vs −2.4 pts (−40.3%) placebo. Body weight: −26.4% (9mg) / −28.7% (−71.2 lbs, 12mg) vs −2.1% placebo. Safety: nausea 38.1–43.2% (vs 10.7% placebo), diarrhea 33.1–34.7% (vs 13.4%), vomiting 20.4–20.9% (vs 0%); AE-driven discontinuation 12.2–18.2% (retatrutide) vs 4.0% (placebo). [Lilly press release, Dec 11, 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lillys-triple-agonist-retatrutide-delivered-weight-loss-of-up-to-an-average-of-71-2-lbs-along-with-substantial-relief-from-osteoarthritis-pain-in-first-successful-phase-3-trial-302638804.html); [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05931367](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05931367) — *note: some secondary trade-press summaries (e.g., Rheumatology Advisor) report slightly different WOMAC percentages (≈−67% / −63%) for the same trial; the figures above are taken directly from Lilly's own press release and should be treated as the canonical numbers, with the discrepancy flagged as unresolved (see Discrepancies below).*
- **ATTAIN-OA PAIN (orforglipron, NCT07153471)** — RECRUITING, not yet reported. Started Sept 15, 2025; ~800 participants; primary completion expected April 2028; completion expected May 2028. No efficacy data yet. Orforglipron itself received its first regulatory approval (U.S., obesity indication only) in April 2026 under the brand Foundayo — the OA indication was not part of that approval. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07153471](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07153471); [PubMed PMID 42479349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42479349/)
- **ENLIGHTEN-4 (eloralintide, NCT07353931)** — RECRUITING, not yet reported. Started Feb 9, 2026; primary completion expected March 2028; completion expected May 2028. No efficacy data yet. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07353931](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07353931)
- **OA07 (LY4065967, NCT07641517)** — RECRUITING, not yet reported. Started June 25, 2026; primary completion expected April 2027. Phase 2, so even a positive result would not be registrational on its own. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07641517](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07641517)
- Orforglipron's U.S. obesity approval and launch (branded Foundayo) actually happened — launched in the U.S. in Q2 2026 per the 10-Q — but strictly for obesity, not OA; useful as a base-rate check on how fast Lilly can convert a positive dataset into an approved/launched product versus where the OA-specific indications currently stand (all still pre-readout or pre-filing). [10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000081/lly-20260630.htm)

## Discrepancies / Watch Items

- **BLA timeline is a "said," not yet a "did."** The Q1 2027 U.S. BLA submission target for retatrutide (bundling obesity + OSA + knee OA pain into one filing) was stated Aug 5, 2026 and has not occurred as of this pull (2026-08-21). Worth re-checking after Q1 2027 to see if it actually happens on schedule, and whether FDA treats the OA claim as part of the base label or requires a separate process.
- **WOMAC percentage discrepancy for TRIUMPH-4.** Lilly's own Dec 11, 2025 press release reports −75.8% (9mg) / −74.3% (12mg) vs −40.3% placebo on the WOMAC pain subscale. At least one secondary/trade outlet reported different numbers (≈−67.2% / −62.6% vs −35.1%) for what appears to be the same trial/endpoint. This pull did not resolve which reflects a different analysis population, visit window, or reporting error — flag for follow-up against the eventual peer-reviewed publication or ClinicalTrials.gov results-tab posting (not yet posted as of this pull).
- **Orforglipron's OA program got materially less promotional treatment than retatrutide's.** The OA trial initiation (Sept 2025) surfaced only in a routine 10-Q, with no standalone Lilly press release found — contrast with retatrutide's OA readout, which got a dedicated, headline press release. This may just reflect that orforglipron's OA trial hasn't read out yet (nothing to promote), but it's also consistent with retatrutide being positioned as Lilly's higher-priority/flagship musculoskeletal-adjacent bet within the GLP-1 class.
- **Eloralintide's OA angle appeared abruptly.** As of the FY2024 10-K (Feb 2025), eloralintide had no OA association at all — it was a Phase 2 obesity-only asset. By the FY2025 10-K (Feb 2026) it was Phase 3 for obesity, and within days (Feb 9, 2026) an OA-specific Phase 3 trial started. The full one-year gap between "no OA program" and "Phase 3 OA trial recruiting" is worth tracking for how the strategic decision was made (no public rationale/announcement was found beyond the bare trial registration and the SEC filings' factual disclosure).
- **LY4065967 has essentially no public trail.** It never appears in six SEC filings checked, and its OA trial (NCT07641517) was first posted only Jun 11, 2026 — the most recent, least-verified data point in this profile. The claimed AT2R mechanism should be treated as unconfirmed until a primary Lilly source (patent, scientific poster, ClinicalTrials.gov mechanism field, or peer-reviewed paper) is found.
- **Framing correction, restated plainly:** the assumption that "these are primarily obesity-indication drugs also being tested in OA populations" is accurate for retatrutide, orforglipron, and eloralintide, but wrong for LY4065967, which has no obesity/weight-loss angle and is instead part of an older, separate Lilly analgesics-testing pipeline (the Chronic Pain Master Protocol) that has cycled through several prior OA-pain candidates without any of them (per our own ClinicalTrials data) yet reaching Phase 3.

## Recent Updates

- **2026-08-22:** A regulatory dispute between Lilly and the FDA over whether retatrutide is legally a "biologic" (BLA, 12-year exclusivity) or qualifies only for NDA treatment (5-year exclusivity) is unresolved and directly affects the OA-bundled filing. The Southern District of Indiana ruled Sept 30, 2025 that retatrutide is not itself a protein for this purpose, but sent FDA's separate "analogous to a protein" theory back to the agency; Lilly has appealed, with oral arguments scheduled Sept 24, 2026 — about six months before the Q1 2027 BLA-pathway submission Lilly has publicly targeted for obesity/OSA/knee-OA. This adds real regulatory-classification risk (and potentially years of exclusivity value) to the timeline already flagged above as a "said, not yet a did." [BioSpace, Aug 2026](https://www.biospace.com/fda/lilly-fda-retatrutide-biologic-dispute-comes-to-a-head-as-submission-nears)
