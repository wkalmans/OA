# UnicoCell Biomed CO. LTD — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** ELIXCYTE (allogeneic adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cell product)
**Mechanism:** Allogeneic (donor-derived) adipose-derived MSCs injected intra-articularly to reduce inflammation and prevent cartilage degradation in knee OA.

## Current Status
UnicoCell Biomed is a Taiwanese stem-cell drug developer with over a decade of focus on this platform. ELIXCYTE's Phase III trial for knee OA completed patient enrollment in November 2025, having received clinical trial approvals from both Taiwan's TFDA (2022) and the U.S. FDA (2023) — a genuinely advanced, well-funded program for a company this size. Earlier-stage data (published in Cytotherapy) showed ELIXCYTE conferred earlier pain and functional improvement than hyaluronic acid, with in vitro work confirming reduced inflammation and cartilage protection. The company also listed on Taiwan's general stock exchange board in October 2025.
