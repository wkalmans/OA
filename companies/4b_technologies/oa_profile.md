# 4B Technologies Limited — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** FB1003
**Mechanism:** TrkA (tropomyosin receptor kinase A) activator; a non-opioid approach to modulating pain signaling.

## Current Status
FB1003 is a non-opioid pain candidate from 4B Technologies (Beijing), positioned as a potential first-in-class TrkA activator. It is in a Phase 1, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study assessing safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics in both healthy volunteers and osteoarthritis pain subjects (NCT06343714), currently recruiting with estimated primary completion in February 2026. No efficacy results or partnership news specific to the OA indication were found beyond the trial registration itself. A separate 4B Technologies asset (4B03-04) has reported Phase 1 chronic-pain data, but that is a distinct compound from FB1003.
