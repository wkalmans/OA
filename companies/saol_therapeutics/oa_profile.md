# Saol Therapeutics Inc — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** SL-1002
**Mechanism:** Chemoneurolytic intra-articular injection using Saol's proprietary CYCLOPHLEX technology, targeting chronic knee pain associated with OA (a local-acting nerve/pain-signal disruption approach rather than a disease-modifying biologic).

## Current Status
Saol Therapeutics (private, with operations in Roswell GA, Dublin, and Bermuda) completed enrollment in its Phase II COMPASS trial for SL-1002 in knee OA pain and, as of an April 2025 announcement, held an FDA End-of-Phase II meeting that the company says provided a clear regulatory path forward; it stated it is eager to begin a pivotal (Phase 3) program. No efficacy data readout has been published yet.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- Saol has advanced beyond the End-of-Phase II meeting: a Phase III randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of SL-1002 for knee OA pain (NCT07226258, target enrollment 150) started in November 2025, confirming the pivotal program is now underway. No efficacy readout has been reported yet. (https://trial.medpath.com/clinical-trial/58c6059577b7b832/nct07226258-sl1002-injectable-knee-pain-osteoarthritis)
