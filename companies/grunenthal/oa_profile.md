# Grünenthal GmbH — Osteoarthritis Profile

*Lighter-touch pull (web/PubMed only — Grünenthal is privately held, no SEC filings). Compiled 2026-08-21.*

## Asset Overview

**RTX-GRT7039 (resiniferatoxin, "RTX")** — a highly potent, intra-articular **TRPV1 (capsaicin-receptor) agonist**. RTX activates TRPV1-expressing sensory neurons and then reversibly "defunctionalizes" the peripheral terminals of C- and A-delta pain fibers, intended to give long-lasting (months) localized analgesia from a single or repeat knee injection without systemic opioid effects. Grünenthal acquired the molecule (also known as MTX-071) via its 2021 acquisition of Swiss biotech Mestex. In vivo it is the same TRPV1 pathway targeted by capsaicin (the active ingredient in Grünenthal's marketed Qutenza patch), just far more potent. Phase 3, knee OA. [Business acquisition/deal background — pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/grunenthal-takes-non-opioid-pain-drug-resiniferatoxin-into-phase-3)

Grünenthal is also the **originator of tapentadol** (Palexia/Nucynta depending on territory), a dual-mechanism opioid/norepinephrine-reuptake-inhibitor analgesic first approved 2008 (US)/2010 (EU), used off-label and in some markets for OA-related pain, but it is not an OA-specific pipeline program — it's a marketed, mature product. [Wikipedia — Tapentadol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapentadol)

## Current Status

- **RTX-GRT7039 Phase 3 program: appears to have failed.** Grünenthal ran three Phase 3 trials under a global program launched August 2022 with NovaQuest Capital Management co-funding the ~1,800-patient program:
  - [NCT05248386](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05248386) — repeated bilateral/unilateral dosing, 469 patients, 52 weeks — Completed, primary completion Nov 18, 2024
  - [NCT05449132](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05449132) — single injection vs. placebo, 466 patients — Completed, primary completion Aug 15, 2024, **results posted**
  - [NCT05377489](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05377489) — open-label safety/tolerability — Completed, primary completion May 15, 2025
  - The posted results for NCT05449132 show only a small, likely non-clinically-differentiated separation from placebo on the primary endpoint (change from baseline in WOMAC Pain at Week 12): **RTX-GRT7039 −3.25 vs. placebo −2.99** (source: ClinicalTrials.gov results module for NCT05449132, fetched 2026-08-21 — p-values/CIs were not visible in what we could extract, so treat the "did not separate" read as directional, not a confirmed p-value).
  - A newly published (Aug 2026) peer-reviewed critical review states plainly that intra-articular TRPV1 agonists in OA — the class RTX-GRT7039 belongs to — **"failed to meet primary endpoints in Phase III trials despite receiving expedited regulatory status"** (RTX-GRT7039 received FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation in May 2023, the only compound in this class with that status). This is the strongest evidence we found that the program missed its primary endpoint. Matta C, et al., *Expert Opinion on Pharmacotherapy*, Aug 2026. PMID: [42373560](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42373560/)
  - Consistent with a failed/discontinued program: **Grünenthal's own current pipeline page (fetched 2026-08-21) no longer lists resiniferatoxin, RTX, or RTX-GRT7039 at all**, despite the company's 2023 guidance targeting a first Marketing Authorization Application submission in 2025 and market entry in 2025/2026. [Grünenthal pipeline page](https://www.grunenthal.com/en/science/pipeline)
  - **We could not find any Grünenthal press release, investor statement, or news report explicitly confirming discontinuation** — this is an inference from (a) the trials completing on schedule with results posted, (b) the small/flat topline numbers on the one trial with public results, (c) the Aug 2026 independent literature review's characterization of the class's Phase 3 failure, and (d) the asset's disappearance from Grünenthal's own pipeline page. Flagging this clearly rather than presenting it as company-confirmed.
- **Broader OA pipeline: none identified beyond RTX-GRT7039.** Grünenthal's currently listed pipeline (Aug 2026) covers pain broadly — an NOP receptor agonist (Phase I complete, Phase II planned 2026), a NaV1.8 sodium-channel inhibitor (entering clinic Q1 2026), and MPC-06-ID/rexlemestrocel-L with partner Mesoblast (Phase III, chronic low back pain from degenerative disc disease) — but none of these are indicated for osteoarthritis specifically. Qutenza (capsaicin 8% patch) is marketed for peripheral neuropathic pain, not OA, and continues to expand via territory licensing deals (South Korea/BCWorld Pharm, Australia/Clinect, early 2026). [Grünenthal pipeline page](https://www.grunenthal.com/en/science/pipeline)

## Key Scientific Literature

- Matta C, et al. "The rise and fall of TRPV1-targeted analgesia in osteoarthritis: a critical appraisal." *Expert Opinion on Pharmacotherapy*, Aug 2026. PMID: [42373560](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42373560/) — concludes intra-articular TRPV1 agonists (the RTX-GRT7039 class) failed Phase III primary endpoints despite expedited regulatory designations; attributes this to reliance on preclinical models that don't capture central sensitization, failure to separate peripheral vs. central pain phenotypes, and composite endpoints in mixed patient populations.
- Tsai EH, et al. "TRPV1 Receptor Modulators: Emerging Therapies for Neuropathic, Osteoarthritic, and Perioperative Pain." *CNS Drugs*, Apr 2026. PMID: [41722000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41722000/)
- Iadarola MJ, et al. "Intraarticular resiniferatoxin, a potent TRPV1 agonist, for treatment of osteoarthritic pain." *Pain Management*, Oct 2025. PMID: [40776673](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40776673/) — notes that as of this review, published human RTX-OA data were still limited to open-label, non-placebo-controlled Phase 1/2 abstracts; does not report Phase 3 outcomes.
- Mobasheri A, et al. "Targeting the TRPV1 pain pathway in osteoarthritis of the knee." *Expert Opinion on Therapeutic Targets*, Oct 2024. PMID: [39450875](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39450875/)
- ACR Meeting Abstract: "An Evaluation of the Efficacy, Pharmacokinetics and Safety of RTX-GRT7039 — a Placebo-controlled Study in Patients with Osteoarthritis Knee Pain." [ACR Abstracts](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/an-evaluation-of-the-efficacy-pharmacokinetics-and-safety-of-the-transient-receptor-potential-vanilloid-1-trpv1-agonist-rtx-grt7039-a-placebo-controlled-study-in-patients-with-osteoarthr/) — earlier-stage (Phase 2) data, positive.
