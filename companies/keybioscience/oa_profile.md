# KeyBioscience AG — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** KBP-336
**Mechanism:** Dual amylin and calcitonin receptor agonist (DACRA), once-weekly injectable, providing weight loss, antinociception, and bone/cartilage-protective effects for metabolic (obesity-associated) OA.

## Current Status
KeyBioscience published preclinical data (Arthritis Research & Therapy, 2024) showing KBP-336 reduced pain and body weight in a meniscectomy/high-fat-diet metabolic OA rat model, positioning it as a "novel disease-modifying osteoarthritis drug." A Phase 2 clinical trial (NCT06833749) is actively recruiting to test KBP-336 for pain in obese individuals with OA and assess quality-of-life, metabolic, and safety effects. No clinical efficacy results have been reported yet.
