# Enlivex Therapeutics Ltd. — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** Allocetra
**Mechanism:** Off-the-shelf allogeneic macrophage-reprogramming cell therapy that shifts macrophages into a homeostatic state to resolve chronic joint inflammation.

## Current Status
Enlivex's Phase I/II trial (NCT06233474) in moderate-to-severe knee osteoarthritis showed a substantial and durable reduction in pain and improved function at six months versus placebo, particularly in the 60+ age group, including reported avoidance of knee replacement surgery in some patients. The company presented this data at OARSI 2025 and was slated to present further data at EULAR 2025. Allocetra is Enlivex's core immunotherapy platform, developed primarily for sepsis and cancer, with OA as a newer indication now moving from Phase I safety run-in into a randomized, placebo-controlled Phase IIa (130+ patients).

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- 2026-03-23: FDA cleared Enlivex's IND for a global, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2b trial of Allocetra in age-related primary moderate-to-severe knee osteoarthritis, based on Phase I/II data in 134 patients showing durable, clinically meaningful benefit through six months (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/23/3260316/0/en/Quality-Longevity-Company-Enlivex-Receives-Investigational-New-Drug-IND-Clearance-from-the-FDA-for-Phase-2b-Trial-of-Allocetra-in-Age-Related-Primary-Moderate-to-Severe-Knee-Osteoa.html).
- Trial has since advanced to Phase IIb, with reporting indicating Danish Medicines Agency approval and first-patient dosing in the U.S. arm of the global Phase 2b program in spring 2026 (https://www.clinicaltrialsarena.com/news/enlivex-advances-lead-cell-therapy-to-phase-iib-in-osteoarthritis/; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001596812/000121390026058130/ea029122901ex99-1.htm).
- Phase IIa 3-month and 6-month data (statistically significant improvement in pain and function vs. placebo) were presented at the OARSI 2026 World Congress, April 23-26, West Palm Beach, FL.
