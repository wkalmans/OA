# TBF Genie Tissulaire — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** SclerFIX-IP
**Mechanism:** Sterile, devitalized, processed umbilical cord amniotic membrane implant placed as joint interposition tissue, targeting hand (PIP joint) osteoarthritis rather than the knee.

## Current Status
TBF Génie Tissulaire is a small, longstanding (est. 1992, ANSM-authorized) French tissue bank near Lyon, not a typical venture-backed biotech. SclerFIX-IP is being evaluated in an active, recruiting, open prospective multicenter trial (NCT06034262) for PIP joint OA in patients who failed conservative treatment and would otherwise face surgery. No results, funding news, or commercial disclosures were found beyond the trial registration — this looks like a niche academic/tissue-bank-sponsored study rather than a commercial drug-development push.
