# OrthoTrophix, Inc — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** TPX-100
**Mechanism:** Intra-articular injection of a peptide fragment of MEPE (matrix extracellular phosphoglycoprotein) intended to activate cartilage-forming cells and slow disease-related bone-shape changes — a disease-modifying (not just symptomatic) approach.

## Current Status
Unlike most companies on this list, OrthoTrophix has real published clinical data. In its earlier Phase 2a randomized, placebo-controlled trial, TPX-100 showed a favorable safety profile and statistically significant improvements in knee function versus placebo; treated knees showed reduced subchondral bone deterioration, and the ~55% of "responder" knees also showed statistically significant improvements in cartilage volume and thickness. A follow-up analysis (Study TPX-100-5, published in Arthritis Research & Therapy) found TPX-100 significantly delayed pathological femoral bone-shape change and stabilized cartilage in moderate-to-severe bilateral knee OA — genuine disease-modification evidence, not just symptom relief. Building on this, OrthoTrophix initiated a Phase 2b trial in mild-to-severe knee OA in the US in May 2025 and announced early completion of enrollment (per a 2026 press release), so a readout should follow. No partnership or financing news was found.
