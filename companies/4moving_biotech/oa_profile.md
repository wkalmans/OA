# 4Moving Biotech — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** 4P004
**Mechanism:** Intra-articular GLP-1 receptor agonist (based on liraglutide) intended to relieve pain and modulate joint biology as a potential disease-modifying OA drug (DMOAD).

## Current Status
4Moving Biotech is a French clinical-stage biotech focused on musculoskeletal disease. 4P004's Phase I LASARE trial (34 knee OA patients, single intra-articular injections 0.3–3.0mg) met its primary safety objective with an adverse-event profile comparable to placebo (June 2024). The program has since gained momentum: FDA cleared the IND for 4P004 in 2026, enabling U.S. expansion of the ongoing Phase 2a INFLAM MOTION trial, and the FDA granted Fast Track designation (April 2026) for knee OA with synovitis after failure of ≥2 prior therapies — a notably positive regulatory signal for a company this size.
