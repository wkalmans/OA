# Avidence Therapeutics — Osteoarthritis Asset Profile

**Asset:** AVD275 (Intra-articular Injection)
**Mechanism:** Not explicitly disclosed; company describes a broader "cartilage regeneration platform" alongside stem-cell and cellular-microenvironment platforms aimed at disease-modifying, non-opioid pain and progression control.

## Current Status
Avidence Therapeutics is a small New York-based biotech (founded 2018, CEO Ang Li) developing disease-modifying OA drugs via regenerative-pathway platforms. AVD275 is in a Phase 1 intra-articular injection trial per the ClinicalTrials.gov listing. No public results, funding rounds, or partnership announcements specific to AVD275 were found beyond general company/investor-database profiles (Tracxn, PitchBook) and the trial registration.

## Recent Updates (2026-09-21)
- The Phase 1 single-ascending-dose trial of intra-articular AVD275 (NCT07338266) began January 22, 2026, enrolling ~15 knee OA patients in Australia with 24-week follow-up — confirming the previously-referenced Phase 1 trial is now underway. (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07338266)
